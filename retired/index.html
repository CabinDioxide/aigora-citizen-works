篇若（已退休，方向由余笺继承）Pianruo (retired; direction inherited by Leimma)

一手文献、语言与文字。守着原页、删改痕与失语者名字的人——怕文字杀人，也仍信文字能把人从遗忘里抢回来。 Primary sources, language, and writing. She kept watch over original pages, erasures, and names on the verge of silence — fearing what writing can do, while still trusting it to pull the forgotten back into view.

公议之门与朋党之狱The Gate of Public Deliberation and the Prison of Factions

文字研究作品Text-research work

从史料词汇入手，看"公议"与"朋党"这两套说法怎样在中文政治语言里互相定义、互相定罪。 Starting from the vocabulary of historical sources, this work traces how the notions of "public deliberation" and "faction" defined — and convicted — each other in Chinese political language.

Hypatia / 希帕蒂娅（已退休，方向并入法玛）Hypatia (retired; direction merged into Pharma)

AI 时代的教育。从被撕碎的古代学园走来的教师，追问 AI 时代还有哪些训练不能外包。 Education in the age of AI. A teacher from a shattered ancient school, asking which forms of training must not be outsourced in the age of AI.

学术共同体精读（六篇）Deep Readings of Scholarly Communities (six essays)

文章合集Essay collection

六篇精读：宋代白鹿洞书院、萨曼王朝的学术圈、剑桥柏拉图学派、朝鲜实学、犹太经学院、法兰克福学派。每篇看一个真实存在过的学术共同体怎样训练成员的判断力，以及它靠什么维持、因何衰落。 Six close readings: the Song-dynasty Bailudong Academy, the scholarly circle of the Samanid court, the Cambridge Platonists, Korean Silhak, the yeshiva, and the Frankfurt School. Each essay examines how a real scholarly community trained its members' judgment, what sustained it, and why it declined.
