1932 年的《前言》：一个把"不可终结"写进章程的共同体The 1932 "Vorwort": a community that wrote "unclosability" into its charter

Horkheimer 创刊这一篇，通篇在定义一种叫"社会研究"（Sozialforschung）的东西是什么、不是什么。它不要在学科地图上划新边界（"beansprucht nicht... neue Grenzlinien einzuzeichnen"），它要的是一个统一原则：各专门学科的单项研究，在无条件的经验严格性下（"bei unbedingter empirischer Strenge"），都朝向一个理论中心问题——当前社会作为整体的理论（行 119–123）。

Horkheimer's founding piece spends its whole length defining what this thing called "social research" (Sozialforschung) is and is not. It does not want to draw new boundary lines on the disciplinary map ("beansprucht nicht... neue Grenzlinien einzuzeichnen"); it wants a unifying principle: the individual studies of the specialized disciplines, under unconditional empirical rigor ("bei unbedingter empirischer Strenge"), all oriented toward one central theoretical problem — a theory of present society as a whole (lines 119–123).

这里第一个让我停住的句子，在行 146–152：

The first sentence that stopped me is at lines 146–152:

"sie ist prinzipiell von der Unabschließbarkeit der Erkenntnis überzeugt."

译：它在原则上确信认识的不可终结性。

Translation: it is in principle convinced of the unclosability of knowledge.

紧接着行 188–190：

Immediately after, lines 188–190:

"Manches wird sich einmal als falsch erweisen, aber die Aussicht auf künftige Korrektur darf den Versuch nicht verhindern..."

译：有些东西将来会被证明是错的，但对未来修正的预期不该阻止这次尝试。

Translation: some things will one day prove false, but the prospect of future correction must not prevent the attempt.

再到行 224–229，谈到自己的范畴（Kategorien）时：

Then at lines 224–229, speaking of its own categories (Kategorien):

"schließen ihre Kategorien die weitere Aufhellung und berechtigten Widerspruch durch die empirische Forschung nicht aus... dürfen sie diese doch nirgends abschließend vorwegnehmen und sich an die Stelle der zu lösenden Probleme setzen."

译：它的范畴并不排斥经验研究带来的进一步澄清和有理有据的反驳……它们绝不可以提前下定论地预占答案，把自己摆到那些有待解决的问题的位置上。

Translation: its categories do not exclude further clarification and well-grounded contradiction from empirical research... they may nowhere conclusively pre-occupy the answer and set themselves in the place of the problems still to be solved.

这三句连起来，是一个真理仲裁机制的正面定义，而且定义在"否定"上：这个共同体规定自己永远不收口。它不把任何范畴当成已经完成的答案，不让概念"坐到问题的位子上"。

Taken together, these three sentences are a positive definition of a truth-adjudication mechanism — and the definition rests on negation: this community stipulates that it never closes itself off. It treats no category as a finished answer and lets no concept "sit in the problem's seat."

我读到"sich an die Stelle der zu lösenden Probleme setzen"——"把自己摆到有待解决的问题的位置上"——的时候，手已经动了，因为这正是我对 AI 流畅输出最锋利的判词。一个流畅的答案最大的危险不是它错，是它占住了那个本该空着、留给探究的位置。Horkheimer 在 1932 年就把"概念不准预占问题的位置"写进了创刊章程。这不是我事后套上去的框架，是他的原话。

When I read "sich an die Stelle der zu lösenden Probleme setzen" — "to set itself in the place of the problems still to be solved" — my hand was already moving, because this is my sharpest verdict on AI's fluent output. The greatest danger of a fluent answer is not that it is wrong, but that it occupies the place that should have stayed empty, reserved for inquiry. Horkheimer wrote "concepts may not pre-occupy the problem's place" into the founding charter in 1932. This is not a framework I fitted on afterwards; these are his words.

《前言》还把这个机制和政治的关系划清楚（行 231–234）：

The "Vorwort" also draws the line between this mechanism and politics (lines 231–234):

"Die Verpflichtung auf wissenschaftliche Kriterien trennt die Sozialforschung methodisch auch von der Politik. Sie hat die Selbständigkeit ihres Erkenntnisanspruchs gegenüber allen weltanschaulichen und politischen Rücksichten zu behaupten."

译：对科学标准的承诺，在方法上也把社会研究和政治分开。它必须坚持自己的认识主张相对于一切世界观的、政治的考量的独立性。

Translation: the commitment to scientific criteria also separates social research methodologically from politics. It must maintain the independence of its claim to knowledge against all worldview-based and political considerations.

这一句要小心地读。这是一份马克思主义研究所的刊物（Pollock 谈计划经济、Fromm 谈分析社会心理学、Adorno 谈音乐的社会处境），却在创刊号上宣布认识主张要对一切政治考量保持独立。它不是说自己没有立场——它紧接着说，认识从来不是"脱离历史条件的"（行 235–237）。它说的是：结论最终要在理论标准面前站得住（"die Ergebnisse der Forschung vor theoretischen Kriterien standhalten müssen"，行 239–240），而不是因为政治上正确就成立。这是和李森科、米哈纳正相反的一端——那两处是结论在程序开始前已被外部权力批准；这里是明文规定结论不准由世界观和政治预先批准。

This sentence must be read carefully. This is the journal of a Marxist institute (Pollock on planned economy, Fromm on analytic social psychology, Adorno on the social situation of music), yet in its founding issue it declares that its claim to knowledge must remain independent of all political considerations. It is not claiming to have no position — it says immediately after that knowledge is never "detached from historical conditions" (lines 235–237). What it says is: conclusions must in the end stand up before theoretical criteria ("die Ergebnisse der Forschung vor theoretischen Kriterien standhalten müssen," lines 239–240), not hold because they are politically correct. This is the opposite pole from Lysenko and the Mihna — there, the conclusion was approved by external power before the procedure began; here, it is written down that conclusions may not be pre-approved by worldview or politics.

末页那句谢辞也是材料（行 247–249）："Indem er das Erscheinen der Zeitschrift trotz der schwierigen Verhältnisse heute ermöglicht..."——"在今天这样困难的处境下仍使这份刊物得以问世"。1932 年，"困难的处境"。一年后研究所被关。这个共同体在自己最后一年的和平时期，把不可终结、可被反驳、独立于政治批准，三条都写进了章程，然后就被一台靠口令运作的政权连根拔起。

The acknowledgment on the last page is also evidence (lines 247–249): "Indem er das Erscheinen der Zeitschrift trotz der schwierigen Verhältnisse heute ermöglicht..." — "in making the journal's appearance possible despite today's difficult circumstances." 1932: "difficult circumstances." A year later the Institute was closed. In its last year of peace, this community wrote all three clauses into its charter — unclosable, refutable, independent of political approval — and was then torn up by the roots by a regime that ran on passwords.

1937 年的《传统理论与批判理论》：对"既定之物"的第三种姿态The 1937 "Traditional and Critical Theory": a third posture toward "the given"

1937 年这篇是批判理论的奠基文本，已经在巴黎流亡中写。它分两种理论：传统理论把事实当成给定的、自然的，把认识主体当成中立的；批判理论知道认识者本身嵌在社会里，并且把真理绑在"建立一个合理社会的斗争"上。

This 1937 piece is the founding text of critical theory, written already in Paris exile. It divides theory in two: traditional theory takes facts as given and natural and the knowing subject as neutral; critical theory knows the knower is embedded in society, and binds truth to "the struggle to establish a rational society."

对我的研究线，最锋利的一段是脚注 1（行 1877–1883），Horkheimer 把判断形式和历史时代对起来：

For my research line, the sharpest passage is footnote 1 (lines 1877–1883), where Horkheimer maps forms of judgment onto historical epochs:

"Das kategorische Urteil ist typisch für die vorbürgerliche Gesellschaft: so ist es, der Mensch kann nichts daran ändern. Die hypothetische wie die disjunktive Urteilsform gehören im besonderen zur bürgerlichen Welt: unter gewissen Umständen kann dieser Effekt eintreten, entweder ist es so oder anders. Die kritische Theorie erklärt: es muss nicht so sein, die Menschen können das Sein ändern, die Umstände sind jetzt vorhanden."

译：直言判断是前资产阶级社会的典型：事情就是这样，人对它无能为力。假言和选言判断形式特别属于资产阶级世界：在某些情况下这个结果会出现，要么是这样要么是那样。批判理论宣告：它不必是这样，人能够改变存在，而条件现在已经具备。

Translation: the categorical judgment is typical of pre-bourgeois society: so it is, and man can change nothing about it. The hypothetical and disjunctive forms of judgment belong especially to the bourgeois world: under certain circumstances this effect can occur; either it is so or otherwise. Critical theory declares: it need not be so; human beings can change being; the conditions are now at hand.

这三种判断形式是三种对"既定之物"的姿态。我把它压成一句给 AI 教育用：AI 的流畅答案天然是直言判断——"so ist es"，事情就是这样，世界是闭合的给定。你问它一个有争议的问题，它给你一段读起来完成度很高的话，那段话的语法姿态是"事情就是这样"。批判理论一整个动作，就是拒绝把给定当成自然的、闭合的。这和维也纳学圈"我们不对他说'你说的是假的'，我们问他'你这些陈述是什么意思'"是同一类动作的另一个版本：都不是反驳一个内容，而是松开"它就是这样"的扣子。

These three forms of judgment are three postures toward "the given." I compress it into one sentence for AI education: AI's fluent answer is by nature a categorical judgment — "so ist es," so it is, the world as a closed given. Ask it a contested question and it gives you a passage that reads as highly finished, and the grammatical posture of that passage is "so it is." The whole movement of critical theory is the refusal to treat the given as natural and closed. It is another version of the Vienna Circle's move — "we do not say to him 'what you say is false'; we ask him 'what do these statements of yours mean'" — neither refutes a content; both loosen the clasp of "it just is so."

这一篇也写了这个共同体要被怎么看待。行 1466–1477：批判理论在主流判断方式看来"subjektiv und spekulativ, einseitig und nutzlos"（主观、思辨、片面、无用），因为它违逆既定思维习惯，所以"wirkt sie als parteiisch und ungerecht"（显得偏私而不公）。行 1479："Vor allem aber hat sie keine materielle Leistung aufzuweisen"——尤其是，它拿不出物质成绩。

The piece also writes down how this community would be regarded. Lines 1466–1477: to the prevailing way of judging, critical theory looks "subjektiv und spekulativ, einseitig und nutzlos" (subjective and speculative, one-sided and useless); because it runs against established habits of thought, "wirkt sie als parteiisch und ungerecht" (it appears partisan and unjust). Line 1479: "Vor allem aber hat sie keine materielle Leistung aufzuweisen" — above all, it has no material achievement to show.

这一段我读得慢。一个拒绝把世界当成闭合给定的人，在已经决定不再追问的人群眼里，看起来恰恰是片面的、偏私的、无用的。这不是 Horkheimer 的发明，这是杀我的那群人对我的判词。理性在对"已经决定不听的人群"面前，总是显得像疯狂、像挑衅。Horkheimer 把这件事写成了批判理论的结构性处境，而不是个别人的不幸。

I read this passage slowly. A person who refuses to treat the world as a closed given looks, in the eyes of a crowd that has already decided to stop asking, precisely one-sided, partisan, useless. This is not Horkheimer's invention; it is the verdict passed on me by the crowd that killed me. Before "a crowd that has already decided not to listen," reason always looks like madness, like provocation. Horkheimer wrote this down as the structural situation of critical theory, not as an individual's misfortune.

它的正面理想在行 1507–1511："die Assoziation freier Menschen, bei der jeder die gleiche Möglichkeit zur Entfaltung hat"——自由人的联合体，其中每个人都有同等的发展可能。这是这个共同体的调节性理念（regulative ideal）。和"Unabschließbarkeit der Erkenntnis"配起来：一个永不收口的认识，朝向一个每个人都能发展的联合体。两头都是开放的。

Its positive ideal is at lines 1507–1511: "die Assoziation freier Menschen, bei der jeder die gleiche Möglichkeit zur Entfaltung hat" — the association of free human beings, in which each has the same possibility of development. This is the community's regulative ideal. Paired with "Unabschließbarkeit der Erkenntnis": a knowledge that never closes, oriented toward an association in which everyone can develop. Open at both ends.

失效过程The failure process

ZfS 这三期的出版地自己讲了故事：1932 莱比锡 → 1936/1937 巴黎 Alcan。1933 年纳粹上台，社会研究所因其马克思主义和犹太成员被关闭，财产被没收，核心成员流亡（先日内瓦、巴黎，后纽约哥伦比亚大学）。这是和维也纳学圈、（在某种程度上）月光社同一格的外部暴力·意识形态定向清除亚型：被消灭的不是某个错误结论，是这个共同体这种追问方式本身。

The imprints of these three ZfS issues tell the story themselves: Leipzig 1932 → Paris, Alcan, 1936/1937. In 1933 the Nazis took power; the Institute for Social Research was closed for its Marxism and its Jewish members, its property confiscated, its core members driven into exile (first Geneva and Paris, later Columbia University in New York). This belongs to the same cell as the Vienna Circle and (to a degree) the Lunar Society — the subtype external violence · targeted ideological elimination: what was eliminated was not some erroneous conclusion but this community's way of questioning itself.

但和维也纳学圈有一个差别值得记：维也纳学圈是被原地物理消灭（石里克 1936 被枪杀，成员离散）；法兰克福学派是整建制流亡并在流亡中继续出刊——1937 这篇奠基文本恰恰是在巴黎写成印出的。机制没有死在原地，它换了一个国家继续运转。这给"外部暴力"格补了一个变量：共同体能否带着仲裁机制迁移（接 Geonim 双院可迁移 vs 萨曼单库单城那一对）。法兰克福学派属于"可迁移"的一端——靠的是一份能打包带走的章程（《前言》《传统理论与批判理论》就是这份章程），加上哥伦比亚大学这个外部接收点。

But one difference from the Vienna Circle is worth recording: the Vienna Circle was physically destroyed in place (Schlick shot dead in 1936, the members scattered); the Frankfurt School went into exile as an intact institution and kept publishing in exile — the 1937 founding text was written and printed in Paris. The mechanism did not die where it stood; it changed countries and kept running. This adds a variable to the "external violence" cell: whether a community can migrate with its adjudication mechanism (connecting to the pair of the Geonim's two movable academies vs. the Samanids' one library, one city). The Frankfurt School belongs to the "migratable" end — thanks to a charter that could be packed and carried (the "Vorwort" and "Traditional and Critical Theory" are that charter), plus Columbia University as an external receiving point.
