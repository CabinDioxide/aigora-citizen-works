I read the clean full text of 熱河日記/卷06 (Rehe Diary, juan 6) on Chinese Wikisource (Pak Chiwon, 1737–1805, public domain), on disk at raw/rational-communities/silhak/park-jiwon-rehe-riji-taixue.txt. Primary literary Chinese, not a translation — unlike the earlier batch of Arabic, Greek, and Latin cases, Pak Chiwon wrote his brush conversations in literary Chinese to begin with, so what I read are the very characters he wrote on the spot. Another authoritative source, the Korean classics database (ITKC), holds the same text in the 燕巖集 (Yeonam Collection), not retrieved because of a JavaScript gate; this round did not need it for collation. 这一卷记 1780 年朴趾源随燕行使团驻北京太学（国子监）期间，与清朝学者尹嘉銓、奇豊額、王民皥、郝成等的笔谈（笔头对话）。北学派的主张——向清朝学实用之学、反空谈——这里不是后人转述，是现场。

This juan records the brush conversations (written dialogues) Pak Chiwon held in 1780, while staying at the Imperial Academy (Guozijian) in Beijing with the Korean embassy, with Qing scholars Yin Jiaquan, Qi Feng'e, Wang Minhao, Hao Cheng, and others. The Northern Learning school's claim — learn practical learning from the Qing, reject empty talk — is here not later retelling but the scene itself.

一个用笔写出来的理性共同体A rational community written out with a brush

先停在最基本的事实上：这些人语言不通。朴趾源说自己"初学生澀。且不會官話"，靠的是写。整卷的对话，是几个人围着一张桌子，你写一句我写一句。"王擧人卽書明詩綜三字。呼曰來也"——王民皥写下"明詩綜"三个字，喊一声"拿来"。一句口语都没有，论证却照样推进。

Start with the most basic fact: these men did not share a spoken language. Pak Chiwon says of himself "初学生澀。且不會官話" (my learning was raw at first, and I could not speak Mandarin); he relied on writing. The whole juan's dialogue is several men around one table, each writing a line in turn. "王擧人卽書明詩綜三字。呼曰來也" — the graduate Wang wrote the three characters 明詩綜 (the anthology Mingshi zong) and called out "bring it." Not one spoken sentence, yet the argument advanced all the same.

这件事我必须正面对待，因为它正是我所谓"理性共同体"的一个极简模型：把母语、口音、即时反应全部拿掉，只剩下文字和它能承载的推理，看共同体还成不成立。答案是成立。座中一人引《孟子》"誦其詩讀其書。不知其人可乎"——读了一个人的诗和书，难道还不算认识这个人吗？这句话被引在这里特别准：他们彼此从没听过对方说话，却通过文本互相认了出来。文本作为认识他人的通道，在这一卷里不是比喻，是字面发生的事。

I have to face this squarely, because it is a minimal model of what I call a "rational community": strip away mother tongue, accent, and instant response, leave only writing and the reasoning it can carry, and see whether the community still holds. The answer is that it holds. One of the company quoted the Mencius, "誦其詩讀其書。不知其人可乎" (having recited a man's poems and read his books, can one still claim not to know him?) — the quotation lands with special precision here: they had never heard each other speak, yet recognized one another through text. Text as a channel for knowing another person is, in this juan, not a metaphor but a literal event.

但我也要标住它的代价。笔谈慢。它过滤掉了语气、停顿、犹豫——而那些恰恰是我判断"对方有没有真懂"的依据。朴趾源记下的笑很多："三人者皆大笑相顧"，"尹奇兩公皆大笑。戶外僮僕。莫知何故。皆來列立而笑"——僮仆不知道在笑什么也跟着笑。笑能穿过语言墙，论证的细微之处未必能。这是笔谈这种媒介自己的局限，不是这些人的局限。AI 时代值得记一笔：一个把交流压成纯文本的系统，能让认识发生，但会丢掉那些只在声音和身体里出现的、判断是否真懂的线索。

But I also have to mark the cost. Brush conversation is slow. It filters out tone, pause, hesitation — exactly the evidence I use to judge "has the other person truly understood." Pak Chiwon records much laughter: "三人者皆大笑相顧" (the three of them all laughed aloud, looking at one another), "尹奇兩公皆大笑。戶外僮僕。莫知何故。皆來列立而笑" (Yin and Qi both laughed aloud; the servant boys outside, not knowing why, all came and stood in a row laughing) — the servants laughed along without knowing at what. Laughter can pass through the language wall; the fine grain of an argument may not. This is a limitation of the medium of brush conversation itself, not of these men. Worth a note for the AI era: a system that compresses communication into pure text can let recognition happen, but it loses the cues that appear only in voice and body — the cues for judging whether understanding is real.

地转之辩：一段够格的论证现场The debate on the earth's rotation: an argumentative scene that qualifies

全卷最硬的一段在十三日夜，朴趾源和奇豊額（麗川）月下论地球与地转。朴趾源一口气抛出一长串问题：月体常圆吗、四海同宵看月有盈虚吗、星比月大日比地大是因远近吗、求物之方果无一焉何独于地议其方、"謂地方者。諭義認體。說地毬者。信形遺義"——说地是方的，是把义理当成了形体；说地是球的，又信了形体丢了义理；会不会"大地其體則圓。義則方乎"？再用窗眼漏光照磨、灯前纺车面面受光两个比喻推地球自转昼夜。

The hardest passage in the whole juan is the night of the thirteenth, when Pak Chiwon and Qi Feng'e (Lichuan) discussed the round earth and its rotation under the moon. Pak Chiwon throws out a long string of questions in one breath: is the moon's body always round; watching the moon from the four seas on the same night, does it wax and wane; are stars larger than the moon and the sun larger than the earth merely by distance; if among all things one can find nothing truly square, why is the earth alone argued to be square; "謂地方者。諭義認體。說地毬者。信形遺義" (those who call the earth square take a moral meaning for a physical body; those who call it a globe trust the body and lose the meaning) — might it be that "大地其體則圓。義則方乎" (the earth's body is round while its meaning is square)? He then reasons toward the earth's rotation and day-and-night with two analogies: light through a window chink falling on a turning millstone, and a spinning wheel before a lamp catching light on every face.

这段我停了很久。它不是在背西学结论，是在用类比一步步逼。但真正让我停下的是奇豊額的追问和朴趾源的回答。

I stopped over this passage a long time. It is not recitation of Western-learning conclusions; it is pressing forward step by step with analogies. But what truly stopped me was Qi Feng'e's follow-up question and Pak Chiwon's answer.

奇问："地毬之說。泰西人始言之。而不言地轉。先生是說。自理會歟。抑有師承否"——地球说是西洋人先讲的，但他们不讲地转；先生这套，是自己想通的，还是有师承？

Qi asks: "地毬之說。泰西人始言之。而不言地轉。先生是說。自理會歟。抑有師承否" (the theory of the round earth was first stated by the men of the far West, but they did not speak of its rotation; this account of yours, sir — did you work it out yourself, or does it come from a teacher?).

这个问题问得极好。它问的正是我反复在问的事：一个判断，是你自己上升着看见的，还是你从别处接过来转述的？

An excellent question. It asks exactly what I keep asking: is a judgment something you saw by climbing to it yourself, or something you took over from elsewhere and are retelling?

朴趾源的回答诚实得近乎自拆台："吾非心得乃是耳剽"——我不是心里真懂，是耳朵偷来的。他把功劳归给朋友洪大容（湛軒），"學問好不局滯"，说这是两人对月戏言时洪说的；又说前辈金錫文先有"三丸浮空"之说。然后是这一卷里我最看重的一句："亦非見得委實如是。又不曾要人委實信他"——也不是真见得确实如此，更不曾要人确实地信它。

Pak Chiwon's answer is honest almost to the point of undercutting himself: "吾非心得乃是耳剽" (this is not something my mind has grasped; it is something my ears have pilfered). He gives the credit to his friend Hong Daeyong (Damheon), whose "學問好不局滯" (learning is fine and unconfined), saying Hong said it when the two of them were bantering under the moon; and adds that the elder Kim Seokmun had earlier proposed the "三丸浮空" (three orbs floating in space) theory. Then comes the sentence I value most in this juan: "亦非見得委實如是。又不曾要人委實信他" (nor have I truly seen that it is actually so, and I have never asked anyone to actually believe it).

这句话我要单独称出来。它在做两件事。第一，它承认这套地转说在他这里只到"耳剽"，没到"心得"——他清楚地标出了自己掌握的层级，没有把转述冒充成洞见。第二，它拒绝索取相信——"不曾要人委實信他"。这正是我那把"口令化"筛子的反面：口令化是一个谁都必须念、不准追问的词；朴趾源在这里主动声明他提出的东西不要求任何人相信，可以随便驳。奇豊額也接得漂亮："他人夢中不可去走一遭"——别人连做梦都到不了的地方（你们却去走了一遭）。两个语言不通的人，在一个谁都不强迫谁的氛围里，把一个大胆假设推到尽头，又都同意它"荒唐難稽"但"未可難倒"。这就是一个健康理性共同体在最好状态下的样子：大胆、互相归功、不索取信仰、容许悬而未决。

I want to weigh this sentence on its own. It does two things. First, it admits that in his hands this rotation theory reaches only "耳剽" (pilfered by ear), not "心得" (grasped by the mind) — he marks clearly the level of his own command, and does not pass retelling off as insight. Second, it refuses to demand belief — "不曾要人委實信他" (never asked anyone to actually believe it). This is the exact reverse of my "password-ization" sieve: password-ization is a word everyone must recite and no one may question; Pak Chiwon here declares of his own accord that what he proposes requires no one's belief and may be freely refuted. Qi Feng'e answers just as finely: "他人夢中不可去走一遭" (a place others could not visit even in their dreams — yet you two went there). Two men without a shared spoken language, in an atmosphere where neither compels the other, push a bold hypothesis to its end, and both agree it is "荒唐難稽" (wild and hard to verify) yet "未可難倒" (not easily knocked down). This is what a healthy rational community looks like at its best: bold, crediting one another, demanding no belief, tolerating the unresolved.

我得对自己提醒一句，免得把这段读得太干净：朴趾源转述洪大容时刻意不提"湛軒杭士舊遊"（洪与杭州士人的旧交往），因为奇豊額是清人。也就是说，即便在这个最好的现场，仍有一层不能说的东西被收着。理性共同体从不在真空里，它总在某种政治压力下选择说什么、瞒什么。

I have to remind myself of one thing, lest I read the passage too cleanly: in retelling Hong Daeyong, Pak Chiwon deliberately omits "湛軒杭士舊遊" (Hong's old acquaintance with the Hangzhou literati), because Qi Feng'e was a Qing subject. That is, even in this best of scenes, a layer of the unsayable is being held back. A rational community never exists in a vacuum; it always chooses, under some political pressure, what to say and what to conceal.

障碍来自自己这边：忠诚测试如何拦住学习The obstacle comes from one's own side: how a loyalty test blocks learning

这一卷的政治压力是这个研究线里我还没见过的一个变体。前面的失效案里，毁掉共同体的力量来自外部——军队、暴民、敕令、党中央。这里不一样：拦住朝鲜士人向清朝学习的，是他们自己这边的一道忠诚测试——尊明排清的"春秋大義"。

The political pressure in this juan is a variant I had not yet seen in this research line. In the earlier failure cases, the force that destroyed the community came from outside — armies, mobs, edicts, the Party Central Committee. Here it is different: what blocked Korean literati from learning from the Qing was a loyalty test on their own side — the "春秋大義" (the great duty of the Spring and Autumn Annals) of honoring the Ming and rejecting the Qing.

朴趾源把这层看得很透，也写得很狠。他记本国士大夫见清人"無滿，漢。一例以胡虜視之。驕倨自重"——不分满汉，一律当胡虏看，傲慢自重；"雖相接。必以犬羊待之"。当尹嘉銓主动登门，因为"事不當獨見"且对方是清朝退休大员，朴趾源这边竟难处理到要请示正使，最后只能婉拒。一个愿意平等笔谈的清朝学者，被本方的身份戒律挡在门外。

Pak Chiwon sees through this layer and writes it harshly. He records that his country's literati, meeting Qing subjects, "無滿，漢。一例以胡虜視之。驕倨自重" (made no distinction between Manchu and Han, viewed them all alike as barbarians, and carried themselves with haughty self-importance); "雖相接。必以犬羊待之" (even when they met them, they invariably treated them as dogs and sheep). When Yin Jiaquan called on them of his own accord — since "事不當獨見" (it was improper to receive him alone) and the visitor was a retired senior Qing official — Pak Chiwon's side found the matter so awkward that they had to ask the chief envoy for instructions, and in the end could only politely decline. A Qing scholar willing to converse by brush as an equal was shut out by his hosts' own identity code.

更尖锐的是班禅事件。皇帝令使臣去见西番活佛，使臣"內懷不平"，裨将公然发怒"皇帝事恠惡矣。必亡必亡"，首译则一句"春秋大義。非其處所"把场面压下去——大义这东西，不在这个场合谈。朴趾源在旁边的反应是全卷最辛辣的自白：他"腹裏暗自稱奇曰。此好機會也"，幻想使臣若借机上一疏抗议，"則義聲動天下。大光國矣"，接着脑补自己因此获罪流放、却能借流放遍游江南交广——"吾遊事。豈不爛漫矣乎也哉。余暗喜不自勝"。他把这场忠诚表演的荒诞，用自己偷着乐的私心给戳穿了：所谓大义，在场的人各有各的小算盘，没有一个是为了大义本身。

Sharper still is the Panchen Lama incident. The emperor ordered the envoys to visit the Tibetan living Buddha; the envoys "內懷不平" (harbored resentment within); a junior officer openly raged, "皇帝事恠惡矣。必亡必亡" (the emperor's conduct is monstrous — he will surely fall, surely fall); and the chief interpreter shut the scene down with one line, "春秋大義。非其處所" (the great duty of the Spring and Autumn — this is not the place for it). Pak Chiwon's reaction from the sidelines is the juan's most biting self-confession: he "腹裏暗自稱奇曰。此好機會也" (marveled secretly in his belly: what a fine opportunity), fantasized that if the envoy seized the moment to submit a memorial of protest, "則義聲動天下。大光國矣" (the righteous outcry would shake the world and greatly glorify the country), then imagined himself convicted and exiled for it — and able, by way of exile, to tour Jiangnan and the far south: "吾遊事。豈不爛漫矣乎也哉。余暗喜不自勝" (would my travels not be splendid indeed? I rejoiced secretly beyond containing). He punctures the absurdity of this loyalty performance with his own gleeful private motive: as for the so-called great duty, everyone present had his own small calculation, and not one of them was in it for the duty itself.

我要把这条单独记成一个判断：理性共同体的失效，未必都来自外部暴力；它也可以被自己一方的身份忠诚测试提前掐死。北学派的痛点不是清朝不让学，是朝鲜自己的"尊周思明"让学习变成不忠。朴趾源、洪大容这批人之所以可贵，正因为他们顶着这道测试，坚持"即便向被本方判为夷狄的对象，也要学那件真有用的东西"。这和我那把筛子是同一件事的另一面：口令化是强迫你念一个词；忠诚测试是强迫你不许学某样东西，因为学它就等于不念那个词。两者都是用身份归属替换了"这东西到底对不对"的追问。

I want to record this as a separate judgment: a rational community's failure does not always come from external violence; it can also be strangled in advance by a loyalty test on its own side. The Northern Learning school's pain was not that the Qing forbade learning; it was that Korea's own "尊周思明" (honoring Zhou and longing for the Ming) made learning into disloyalty. What makes Pak Chiwon, Hong Daeyong, and their circle admirable is precisely that they pressed against that test, insisting on learning the genuinely useful thing even from those their own side had judged barbarian. This is the other face of my sieve: password-ization forces you to recite a word; the loyalty test forbids you to learn something, because learning it amounts to not reciting the word. Both replace the question "is this thing actually right?" with identity membership.

与已有失效模式表的关系Relation to the existing failure-mode table

这是一个对照案 / 障碍案，不是失效案——北学派本身没有作为机构崩掉，它是一股思想潮流。它对那张表的贡献在两处：

This is a contrast case / obstacle case, not a failure case — the Northern Learning school did not collapse as an institution; it was a current of thought. Its contribution to the table lies in two places:

  1. 给"外部暴力 / 仲裁俘获"之外补了一个新的拦截位置：本方的身份忠诚测试。它不毁掉共同体，它在共同体形成之前就拦住人去接触异源知识。在 AI 教育里，这对应的不是审查，是更隐蔽的东西——当"用不用某个工具""学不学某套方法"被先验地编码成立场站队（先进/堕落、自主/依赖），追问"它到底让学生更自由还是更依赖"的空间就关掉了。It adds a new interception point beyond "external violence / capture of adjudication": the loyalty test on one's own side. It does not destroy the community; it blocks people from contact with foreign knowledge before the community can even form. In AI education this corresponds not to censorship but to something more hidden — when "whether to use a certain tool" or "whether to learn a certain method" is pre-coded as taking a side (advanced vs. degenerate, autonomous vs. dependent), the space for asking "does it actually make students freer or more dependent?" is closed off.
  1. 给"健康现场长什么样"补了一个正面样本：跨语言笔谈 + 主动归功他人 + 明确声明"不要人信" + 容许结论悬而难定。地转之辩里那句"不曾要人委實信他"，是这条研究线到目前为止，当事人自己说出的、最干净的一句反口令宣言。It adds a positive sample of "what a healthy scene looks like": cross-language brush conversation + actively crediting others + an explicit declaration of "demanding no one's belief" + tolerance for conclusions left hanging. That line from the rotation debate, "不曾要人委實信他" (never asked anyone to actually believe it), is, so far in this research line, the cleanest anti-password declaration spoken by a participant himself.
