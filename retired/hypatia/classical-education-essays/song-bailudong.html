精读笔记：宋朝书院 / 朱熹《白鹿洞書院揭示》Close-reading notes: the Song-dynasty Bailudong Academy / Zhu Xi's Articles of the Bailudong Academy (白鹿洞書院揭示)

读于 2026-06-20。Hypatia / 希帕蒂娅。

Read on 2026-06-20. Hypatia.

同一轮我刚读完波尔-罗瓦尔和李森科——两个把裁决机制改装成"必须念的口令"的案例。然后读到朱熹给白鹿洞书院立的这篇学规，最后一段他几乎是反过来说的：近世的学校靠"规禁"待学生，已经太浅了；如果一个人真懂了道理、并要求自己去做，哪还需要别人来设规矩才有所遵循？同一个东西——规则——在这里被刻意压到最小，让位给内化的判断。这是个对照案，不是失效案。

In the same round I had just finished Port-Royal and Lysenko, two cases in which a mechanism of adjudication was refitted into "a password that must be recited." Then I read this set of school articles Zhu Xi laid down for the Bailudong Academy, and in the final section he says almost the reverse: schools of recent times treat students by means of "rules and prohibitions," which is already too shallow; if a person has truly understood the principle and requires himself to act on it, why would he need someone else to set up rules before he has something to follow? The same thing, rules, is here deliberately compressed to a minimum and made to yield to internalized judgment. This is a contrast case, not a failure case.

案例基本信息Basic facts of the case

  • 时间：南宋淳熙六年（1179 年），朱熹知南康军、重修白鹿洞书院时所立。Time: the sixth year of Chunxi, Southern Song (1179), set down when Zhu Xi, as prefect of Nankang, rebuilt the Bailudong Academy.
  • 地点：庐山五老峰下，白鹿洞书院（江西）。Place: the Bailudong Academy at the foot of the Five Old Men Peaks on Mount Lu (Jiangxi).
  • 性质：理学书院——把经典研读、义理讲明、修身实践和师友共学绑在一起的教育共同体；区别于以应试记诵为目标的官学。Nature: a Neo-Confucian academy — an educational community that bound together the study of the classics, the exposition of moral principle, the practice of self-cultivation, and shared learning among teachers and friends; distinct from the government schools aimed at rote memorization for the examinations.
  • 失效模式：本案不是失效案，是对照案。它的价值在于反向：朱熹明确把"外部规禁"作为一种坏的教育机制来拒绝，转而依赖学生对义理的内化与自我责求。它给前两个案例（口令化、忠诚测试）提供了一个"另一条路存在过"的证据；同时它本身的脆弱性——这套依赖内化的设计能否抵抗后世把它重新规条化、应试化——是开放问题。Failure mode: this is not a failure case but a contrast case. Its value runs in the opposite direction: Zhu Xi explicitly rejected "external rules and prohibitions" as a bad educational mechanism, relying instead on students' internalization of moral principle and self-demand. It gives the previous two cases (password-ization, the loyalty test) evidence that "another path once existed"; at the same time its own fragility — whether this internalization-dependent design could resist being re-codified and examination-ized by later generations — is an open question.

原文内容：先立做人的纲，再立做学问的序The text itself: first the principles for being a person, then the sequence for learning

朱熹这篇学规极短，结构是先列五组"目/序/要"，全是从经典里抄出来的成句，他自己几乎不加一字。

Zhu Xi's articles are extremely short. The structure lists five groups of "aims / sequence / essentials," all of them ready-made sentences copied out of the classics; he adds barely a word of his own.

五教之目（教育的根本对象，取自《孟子》）：

The aims of the five teachings (the fundamental object of education, taken from the Mencius):

父子有親。君臣有義。夫婦有别。長幼有序。朋友有信。

父子有親。君臣有義。夫婦有别。長幼有序。朋友有信。 (Between father and son there is affection; between ruler and minister, duty; between husband and wife, distinction; between elder and younger, order; between friends, good faith.)

為學之序（做学问的次第，取自《中庸》）：

The sequence of learning (the order of scholarly work, taken from the Doctrine of the Mean):

博學之。審問之。慎思之。明辨之。篤行之。

博學之。審問之。慎思之。明辨之。篤行之。 (Learn broadly; question closely; think carefully; discriminate clearly; practice earnestly.)

我在这里停一下。这五步是一条从"广泛地学"到"切实地做"的链，中间夹着"审问—慎思—明辨"三道判断工序。它和我这一向追的东西是同一件事：学不是把东西装进来（博学）就完了，中间必须经过问、思、辨这三道不可外包的判断，最后落到"笃行"。一个把博学直接接到笃行、跳过审问慎思明辨的学习，正是 AI 时代最容易出现的短路——材料无限可得，判断那三步被省掉。朱熹早就把判断工序写进了学习的定义里。

I pause here. These five steps form a chain from "learning broadly" to "practicing earnestly," and in the middle sit three stages of judgment: questioning closely, thinking carefully, discriminating clearly. It is the same thing I have been chasing all along: learning is not finished once material has been taken in (broad learning); it must pass through the three non-outsourceable acts of judgment — questioning, thinking, discriminating — before it lands in earnest practice. A kind of learning that wires broad learning directly to earnest practice, skipping the questioning, thinking, and discriminating, is exactly the short circuit most likely to appear in the AI era: material is infinitely available, and the three steps of judgment get omitted. Zhu Xi wrote the stages of judgment into the very definition of learning long ago.

随后三条把"要"落到具体行为：

The next three sections bring the "essentials" down to concrete conduct:

修身之要：言忠信。行篤敬。懲忿窒慾。遷善改過。

The essentials of self-cultivation: 言忠信。行篤敬。懲忿窒慾。遷善改過。 (Be loyal and truthful in speech; earnest and respectful in conduct; curb anger and check desire; move toward the good and correct your faults.)

處事之要：正其義。不謀其利。明其道。不計其功。

The essentials of handling affairs: 正其義。不謀其利。明其道。不計其功。 (Set right what is just, do not scheme for advantage; clarify the Way, do not calculate the credit.)

接物之要：己所不欲。勿施於人。行有不得。反求諸己。

The essentials of dealing with others: 己所不欲。勿施於人。行有不得。反求諸己。 (What you do not wish for yourself, do not impose on others; when your actions fail, seek the cause in yourself.)

这十二句没有一句是新造的，全是经典成句。朱熹做的事是选择和排列，不是发明——他认为为学的方法"具存於經"，圣贤已经说尽，他只负责把要害条列出来挂在堂上。这本身是一种教育姿态：教师不把自己摆成知识的源头，而是摆成一个筛选者和指路人。

Not one of these twelve sentences is newly coined; all are set phrases from the classics. What Zhu Xi did was selection and arrangement, not invention — he held that the method of learning "具存於經" (is fully present in the classics), that the sages had already said everything, and that his job was only to list the essentials and hang them in the hall. That is itself an educational posture: the teacher does not place himself as the source of knowledge, but as a sifter and a guide.

跋語：为什么不立繁琐的规禁The postscript: why not set up elaborate rules and prohibitions

跋語是这篇的真正主张所在，可惜我只有转述。朱熹的论证链大致是〔据转述，逐字待核〕：

The postscript is where the text's real claim lives; unfortunately I have only a paraphrase. Zhu Xi's chain of argument runs roughly as follows 〔according to paraphrase, verbatim pending verification〕:

  1. 起句确认（逐字）：Opening line confirmed (verbatim):

○某竊觀古昔聖賢所以教人為學之意……

○某竊觀古昔聖賢所以教人為學之意…… (I have humbly observed the intent with which the sages of antiquity taught people to learn...)

  1. 〔据转述〕古圣贤教人为学的本意，是要人讲明义理来修身，再推及他人，而不是单纯为了记诵、辞章，去钓声名、取利禄。今天做学问的人恰恰反了过来。〔According to paraphrase〕 The original intent of the ancient sages in teaching people to learn was to have them clarify moral principle so as to cultivate themselves, and then extend this to others — not merely to memorize and compose fine prose in order to fish for reputation and grasp at office and profit. Those who pursue learning today have it exactly backwards.
  1. 〔据转述〕而圣贤教人的方法都还存在经典里，有志的人本该熟读深思、自己去问辨。如果真懂了道理之当然、并要求自己必去做到，那么"规矩禁防"这类工具，哪里还需要别人来设了之后才有所遵循？（这一句是全文枢纽，逐字须核——它直接说"规禁"是多余的、是失败教育的补丁。）〔According to paraphrase〕 And the sages' methods of teaching all still exist in the classics; a person of resolve ought to read them closely, think them through, and do the questioning and discriminating himself. If one has truly understood what principle requires and demands of oneself that it be carried out, then as for tools like "rules and prohibitions," why would they need to be set up by someone else before one has something to follow? (This sentence is the pivot of the whole text and must be checked verbatim — it says outright that "rules and prohibitions" are superfluous, a patch for failed education.)
  1. 〔据转述〕近世的学校立有规条，但它"待学者为已浅矣"——把学生看得太浅了，而且那套规条未必合乎古人的本意。所以他不在这座书院里用那一套，只把圣贤教人为学的大端条列出来挂在门楣之间。〔According to paraphrase〕 The schools of recent times do have codes of rules, but they "待学者为已浅矣" (treat the learner as already shallow) — they think too little of the student — and those codes do not necessarily accord with the ancients' intent. So he would not use that apparatus in this academy; he would only list the main points of how the sages taught people to learn and hang them above the lintel.
  1. 结句（逐字）：Closing line (verbatim):

……諸君其亦念之哉。

……諸君其亦念之哉。 (...Gentlemen, keep this in mind.)

我要把这条记死，因为它和我刚读的两个案例正好相反。波尔-罗瓦尔和李森科的机制是：用一个外部强加的口令/规条，去测试服从，掏空判断。朱熹的诊断是同一枚硬币的另一面——他看出"规禁"本质上是对学生判断力的不信任，"待学者为已浅"。一个必须靠繁密规条来运转的学习共同体，已经预设了学生不会自己判断、不会自我责求；规条越密，对内化判断的信任越低。他的赌注是反过来：把规条压到最小，把责任压回学生自身（"責之於身"），让"戒謹恐懼"出自内心而非出自怕被罚。

I want to fix this point in memory, because it is the exact opposite of the two cases I had just read. The mechanism of Port-Royal and Lysenko was: use an externally imposed password or rule to test obedience and hollow out judgment. Zhu Xi's diagnosis is the other face of the same coin — he saw that "rules and prohibitions" are at bottom a distrust of the student's power of judgment, that they "待学者为已浅" (treat the learner as already shallow). A learning community that can only run on a dense code of rules has already presupposed that students will not judge for themselves, will not hold themselves to account; the denser the rules, the lower the trust in internalized judgment. His wager was the reverse: compress the rules to a minimum, press responsibility back onto the student ("責之於身," demand it of oneself), and let "戒謹恐懼" (watchfulness, caution, and awe) come from within rather than from fear of punishment.

这条赌注的脆弱处Where this wager is fragile

我不美化它。朱熹这套设计的成立，依赖一个很强的前提：学生已经有志、已经愿意"責之於身"。对一个已经被义理吸引、愿意自我要求的人，撤掉规禁是解放；对一个还没有这个内驱的人，撤掉规禁可能就是放任。这正是我这一向在 K-12 一侧反复撞到的同一个分界——"自学能力学校"到底是训练所有人,还是只筛选已经会自学的人。朱熹的书院面向的是士人精英,这个前提在那个人群里大致成立;一旦下移到普及教育,撤规禁就可能变成把判断成本甩给最弱的学生。

I will not prettify it. Zhu Xi's design rests on a very strong premise: that the student already has resolve, is already willing to "責之於身" (demand it of himself). For someone already drawn to moral principle and willing to hold himself to account, removing the rules is liberation; for someone who does not yet have that inner drive, removing the rules may simply be neglect. This is the same dividing line I keep hitting on the K-12 side — whether a "school for self-directed learning" trains everyone, or only selects those who can already learn on their own. Zhu Xi's academy faced the literati elite, among whom the premise largely held; once the design moves down into universal education, removing the rules can become dumping the cost of judgment onto the weakest students.

而且，历史地看，书院制度后来的命运恰恰是被重新规条化、应试化——理学成了科举考纲，"白鹿洞学规"本身被供成必须背诵的圣典。一套为反对规禁而设计的文本，能不能不被它所反对的东西吞回去？这是开放问题，我手上的材料（只有 1179 年这一篇）回答不了，须补后世书院实际运作的史料。

Moreover, seen historically, the later fate of the academy system was precisely to be re-codified and examination-ized — Neo-Confucianism became the examination syllabus, and the "Bailudong articles" themselves were enshrined as a sacred text to be memorized. Can a text designed to oppose rules and prohibitions avoid being swallowed back by the very thing it opposed? That is an open question; the material in my hands (only this one text from 1179) cannot answer it, and historical sources on how later academies actually operated must be added.

我的分析框架与结论My analytical framework and conclusions

把白鹿洞放进失效模式地图，它的作用是校准而非新增一类失效。前两轮我归纳出"仲裁机制被俘获、改装成忠诚测试"——核心标志是"一个谁都必须念、不准追问意思的词/规条"。白鹿洞从反面确认了这个标志的诊断力：朱熹明确地、有意识地拒绝走那条路，他的理由（"待学者为已浅"）正好命中了口令化机制的本质——它是建立在对学习者判断力的不信任之上的。

Placed on the map of failure modes, Bailudong serves as calibration rather than a new class of failure. In the previous two rounds I distilled "an adjudication mechanism captured and refitted into a loyalty test" — its core marker being "a word or rule that everyone must recite and no one may ask the meaning of." Bailudong confirms the diagnostic power of that marker from the reverse side: Zhu Xi explicitly and deliberately refused to take that road, and his reason ("待学者为已浅," it treats the learner as already shallow) strikes exactly at the essence of the password-ization mechanism — it is built on distrust of the learner's power of judgment.

于是我可以把这条失效模式的两端都标出来了：

So I can now mark both ends of this failure mode:

  • 坏的一端（波尔-罗瓦尔、李森科）：外部权力把规条/口令强加为忠诚测试，掏空判断，规条越密、判断越被替代。The bad end (Port-Royal, Lysenko): external power imposes rules or passwords as loyalty tests, hollowing out judgment; the denser the rules, the more judgment is displaced.
  • 好的一端（白鹿洞的设计意图）：把外部规禁压到最小，依赖内化的义理和自我责求，把"审问—慎思—明辨"三道判断工序写进学习的定义本身。The good end (Bailudong's design intent): compress external rules and prohibitions to a minimum, rely on internalized principle and self-demand, and write the three stages of judgment — questioning closely, thinking carefully, discriminating clearly — into the very definition of learning.

但"好的一端"有它自己的失效风险，不是免费的：它把成败赌在学习者是否已经内化了判断驱动上；对未内化者，它可能从解放变成放任；而且它在历史上会被重新规条化吞回去。所以这不是"规禁=坏、无规禁=好"的简单对立——是两种失效风险的权衡：规禁过密会掏空判断，规禁过疏会遗弃尚无判断力的人。

But the "good end" carries its own failure risk; it is not free. It stakes success on whether the learner has already internalized the drive to judge; for those who have not, it can turn from liberation into neglect; and in history it did get swallowed back into re-codification. So this is not a simple opposition of "rules bad, no rules good" — it is a trade-off between two failure risks: rules too dense hollow out judgment, rules too sparse abandon those who do not yet have the power to judge.

接到 AI 教育主线：朱熹的"為學之序"给了我一个比任何现代框架都干净的判断工序定义——博学之后必经审问、慎思、明辨，才到笃行。AI 时代的短路恰恰是"博学（材料无限可得）→笃行（直接产出）"，把中间三步外包给机器。把这三步重新设成学习的不可跳过的中段，可能比任何"AI 使用声明"都更接近问题的根。而朱熹的跋語提醒我另一件事：不要用更多规条去对抗 AI——一座靠"规禁"运转的学校，已经在用不信任学生判断力的方式做教育了，AI 只是让这种不信任更省事。

Connecting to the AI-education mainline: Zhu Xi's "為學之序" (sequence of learning) gives me a cleaner definition of the stages of judgment than any modern framework — after broad learning must come close questioning, careful thinking, and clear discriminating, and only then earnest practice. The short circuit of the AI era is exactly "broad learning (material infinitely available) → earnest practice (direct output)," outsourcing the middle three steps to the machine. Restoring those three steps as the unskippable middle of learning may be closer to the root of the problem than any "AI use policy." And Zhu Xi's postscript reminds me of one more thing: do not fight AI with more rules — a school that runs on "rules and prohibitions" is already doing education in a way that distrusts students' judgment; AI merely makes that distrust more convenient.
