这是一次完整运行的历史模拟。世界从公元前四千年的两千一百万人开始，粮食、人口、耕地、农业技术、政体、战争和瘟疫分别按自己的规则逐期演算，直到公元 1500 年。下面的动画每十期截取一帧，可切换八个图层，也可拖动时间轴查看它们的变化。 One completed run of a historical simulation. The world starts in 4000 BC with about twenty-one million people; food, population, cropland, farming technique, polities, war, and epidemics each advance by their own rules, period after period, through to AD 1500. The animation below samples every tenth period; switch between eight layers and drag the timeline to watch each one change.

这一版做了什么What changed in v4

此前版本中，只要格子里有可耕地，第一期就可以种地，因此美洲、撒哈拉以南非洲和两河流域在公元前四千年同时出现农民。这一版改为按照作物驯化年代开始农业。起点依据 ArchaeoGLOBE：这项数据集汇集全球考古学家对各地古代土地利用的评估。距今六千年时，农业已属“常见”以上的 24 个地区，共 9,570 格，从第一期就有农业；中美洲、萨赫勒、苏丹东部、印度南部、安第斯高原和北美东部这六处独立驯化中心，则要到各自的考古年代才在当地出现农业。其他地方的人只能采集渔猎，等农业从相邻格子传来，不能自行发明农业。 In earlier versions any cell with cultivable land could farm from the first period, so the Americas, sub-Saharan Africa, and Mesopotamia all had farmers in 4000 BC. This version starts agriculture by the dates of crop domestication. The starting map comes from ArchaeoGLOBE, a dataset that collects archaeologists' assessments of ancient land use around the world: the 24 regions where farming was already at least "common" six thousand years ago — 9,570 cells — farm from the first period, while six independent centers of domestication (Mesoamerica, the Sahel, the eastern Sudan, southern India, the Andean highlands, and eastern North America) appear in place at their archaeological dates. Everyone else lives by foraging until farming arrives from a neighboring cell; they cannot invent it themselves.

农业只从接壤的格子传入，不经过模型中的远距离商路和海路。定耕农业的扩散，依靠农民一代代迁往邻近土地，或依靠附近居民学会种地，并不是商人把作物带到千里之外。按这种方式传播，农业在欧洲的速度约为每年 0.9 公里，接近欧洲新石器时代扩散的实测速度（每年约一公里）。 Farming now spreads only between adjacent cells, never along the model's long-distance trade and sea routes, because settled agriculture spread as farmers moved onto neighboring land generation after generation, or as their neighbors took it up — not as merchants carried a crop a thousand kilometers. Spreading this way it advances across Europe at about 0.9 km a year, close to the measured pace of the European Neolithic expansion, roughly one kilometer a year.

瘟疫的起点也作了调整。天花和麻疹只能在人口足够多的地方起源：一个格子的人口超过某个数，病毒才能不断找到新的易感者，而不至于自行消失。按传播规律推算，天花的门槛是 6.7 万人，麻疹是 11 万人。超过门槛的格子每期有千分之一的概率成为起源地；千分之一这个概率没有出处，是三档试算中的中间一档。结核不需要大人群，也能长期潜伏在人体内，因此改为每格每期一千万分之一的起源概率；全世界平均约一千年出现一次，这个数同样没有出处。 Epidemics now start differently too. Smallpox and measles can arise only where population is large enough: a cell must hold more than a certain number of people for the virus to keep finding new susceptible hosts instead of burning out. Worked out from transmission theory, that number is 67,000 for smallpox and 110,000 for measles. Each cell above the threshold has a one-in-a-thousand chance per period of becoming an origin; that probability has no source and is the middle of three trial values. Tuberculosis can lie latent for years without a large population, so it instead gets an origin probability of one in ten million per cell per period — about once a millennium worldwide — a figure that likewise has no source.

人口为什么两次崩溃Why population collapsed twice

模拟开始后的五百年里，有农业的地区人口增长很快。第 102 期（公元前 3485 年），全世界人口达到 2.81 亿，其中欧洲一地就有 1.13 亿，而 HYDE 对公元前三千年全世界人口的估计只有 4,300 万。增长如此之快，是因为这些地区已有农业，却还没有只能在大人群中存活的传染病；模型中没有其他机制提高死亡率。 In the first five hundred years, population in the farming regions grew very fast. By period 102 (3485 BC) the world held 281 million people, 113 million of them in Europe alone, while HYDE puts the whole world at 43 million in 3000 BC. It grew this fast because these places had farming but not yet the diseases that survive only in large populations, and nothing else in the model pushed their death rates up.

下一期，即公元前 3480 年，天花在尼罗河谷上游（北纬 26.4°、东经 32.0°）起源，随后传遍旧大陆。到公元前 2995 年，世界人口降至 6,400 万；公元前 2495 年只剩 3,500 万。天花在公元前 290 年从模拟中消失，但旧大陆人口始终没有回升：公元元年旧大陆只有 1,620 万人，HYDE 的估计是 2.07 亿。原因尚未查清。结核在公元前 2210 年于蒙古高原（北纬 44.5°、东经 100.2°）起源，此后长期存在于旧大陆约四万个格子中，美洲一格也没有，整个模拟共造成 3.34 亿人死亡。旧大陆始终未能回升，美洲却持续增长；两边最明显的差别是有没有结核，因此结核是首先要查的一项。 The next period, 3480 BC, smallpox arose in the upper Nile valley (26.4° N, 32.0° E) and spread across the Old World. World population fell to 64 million by 2995 BC and to 35 million by 2495 BC. Smallpox disappeared from the simulation in 290 BC, yet Old World population never recovered: in AD 1 the Old World held 16.2 million people against HYDE's 207 million. The cause has not been traced. Tuberculosis arose on the Mongolian plateau (44.5° N, 100.2° E) in 2210 BC and stayed resident in some forty thousand Old World cells thereafter — not one in the Americas — killing 334 million people over the run. The plainest difference between an Old World that never recovered and an Americas that kept growing is whether tuberculosis was present, so it is the first thing to examine.

美洲的情形相反。当地农业要到各地的驯化年代才出现，旧大陆的天花没有使美洲人口下降：公元前 3495 年到前 2995 年，美洲人口从 690 万增至 910 万。此后人口持续增长：公元元年为 5,140 万，是 HYDE 对美洲估计（1,800 万）的 2.9 倍；公元 1055 年达到 2.28 亿，占全世界的 93%。公元 1085 年，天花再次起源。此时距天花上一次消失已有一千三百多年，模型中没有任何人对它有免疫力，五年内就有 8,526 个格子出现病例，这一波损失的人口九成在美洲。到公元 1500 年，世界人口只剩 2,640 万，是 HYDE 的百分之五。 The Americas went the other way. Farming appeared there only at each region's domestication date, and the Old World's smallpox did not bring American numbers down: between 3495 and 2995 BC the Americas grew from 6.9 to 9.1 million. Population then kept growing: 51.4 million in AD 1, 2.9 times HYDE's estimate for the Americas (18 million), and 228 million by AD 1055 — 93 percent of the world. In AD 1085 smallpox arose again. It had been gone from the simulation for more than thirteen centuries and no one in the model had any immunity; within five years 8,526 cells had cases, and nine-tenths of the population lost in this wave was in the Americas. By AD 1500 the world held 26.4 million people, five percent of HYDE's figure.

其他还没对上的地方Other mismatches

麻疹在整个模拟中没有起源。根据每十期一次的快照估算，人口超过 11 万的格子累计只有约一千个“格子乘期数”的机会；每次概率为千分之一，期望起源约一次，而一次也没有发生的可能约为三分之一，这次恰好没有发生。 Measles never arose. Estimated from snapshots taken every ten periods, cells above 110,000 people added up to only about a thousand cell-periods of opportunity; at one in a thousand each, the expected number of origins is about one, and the chance of none at all is about a third. This run drew none.

东南亚和日本的农业到得太早。模型中，日本在公元前 3535 年、东南亚在公元前 3465 年就有了定耕农业，考古记录显示两地都要晚一千年以上。农业在模型中只从相邻格子传入，因此问题可能出在起点划定，或格子之间的接壤关系上，具体是哪一处还没有查。 Farming reaches Southeast Asia and Japan too early. In the model Japan has settled agriculture by 3535 BC and Southeast Asia by 3465 BC; in the archaeological record both come more than a thousand years later. Since farming in the model spreads only between adjacent cells, the error lies either in how the starting regions were drawn or in which cells count as neighbors; which of the two has not yet been checked.

八个图层各是什么The eight layers

政体归属：每个国家一种颜色，深蓝底色的是无人统治的格。人口：每格人数按对数着色。耕地：每格熟地面积。采集者：每格依靠采集渔猎生活的人数，标题中给出他们占世界人口的比例。口粮比值：每人每天分得的口粮相对于温饱线的比例，1 是刚够吃，红色表示饥荒。城市：城内人口。记录手段：已有文字或其他记账手段的格。官僚：在编官吏按治下格摊算。 Polities: one hue per state; cells left in the dark blue base color are under no rule. Population: people per cell, shaded logarithmically. Cropland: cultivated area per cell. Foragers: people per cell living by hunting, gathering, and fishing, with their share of world population in the title. Food ratio: daily ration per person against the subsistence line, where 1 is just enough and red is famine. Cities: urban population. Record-keeping: cells that have writing or another means of keeping accounts. Bureaucracy: officeholders spread across the cells they govern.

怎么读它How to read it

这是模型，不是历史。它回答的是“给定这套规则，会出现什么样的世界”，不能回答“历史为什么是这样”。看动画时，应先看形态，不必盯住具体数字：农业从几个中心向外扩散；人口先增长，被天花压低，在美洲回升之后又被天花压低一次；政体从无到有，从零星分布到密布。与真实历史不相符的地方，说明模型缺少某种机制，偏差本身就是线索。 This is a model, not history. It can answer what kind of world grows out of a given set of rules; it cannot answer why history went the way it did. What is worth watching is shape rather than exact numbers: farming spreading outward from a few centers; population rising, being cut down by smallpox, recovering in the Americas, and being cut down again; polities appearing from nothing, first scattered and later dense. Where it fails to match the real record, that gap is itself useful — it points to a mechanism the model is missing.
