这是一次跑完的历史模拟。世界从公元前四千年的一千六百万人起步，粮食、人口、耕地、技术、政体、战争按各自的规则逐期推演，跑到公元 1500 年时是 4.27 亿人、1,142 个政体。下面的动画每十期取一帧，可以切换八个图层，拖动时间轴看它们各自怎么变。 One completed run of a historical simulation. The world starts in 4000 BC with about sixteen million people; food, population, cropland, technology, polities, and war each advance by their own rules, period after period. By AD 1500 it holds 427 million people in 1,142 polities. The animation below samples every tenth period; switch between eight layers and drag the timeline to watch each one change.

耕地单位是万平方公里。HYDE 是荷兰环境评估署的全球历史人口与土地利用数据库。这一版里它不再只是事后对照：可垦上限随技术档抬高的那组系数，是照着 HYDE 的耕地序列标定出来的（见下一节）。人口那三列没有参与标定，是模型自己跑出来的。 Cropland is in units of 10,000 km². HYDE is the Netherlands Environmental Assessment Agency's history database of the global environment. In this version it is no longer only an after-the-fact check: the coefficients by which technology raises the cultivable ceiling were calibrated against the HYDE cropland series (see the next section). The population columns took no part in that calibration and are what the model produced on its own.

哪些数是标定出来的Which numbers were calibrated

这个项目有一条纪律：模型参数不许用历史结果标定，否则模型"对上历史"就不再说明任何事情。这一版里有一处破例，写在这里而不是藏起来。 This project keeps one rule: parameters must not be calibrated on historical outcomes, because a model tuned that way tells you nothing when it then "matches history." This version makes one exception, stated here rather than buried.

破例的是"一格里有多少地开得动"这个系数。模型的纯地理可垦上限是 1,250 万平方公里，那是二十世纪的机械与化肥能种的地；而 HYDE 说公元 1500 年全世界只开垦了 248 万。中间差的是"石器和铁器时代的人到底开得动多少地"。这条判据在农学文献里取不到——取料回件确认过，文献不按"工具 → 可耕上限"这个形式表述。所以这组系数（按金属档五级：0.016、0.05、0.10、0.18、0.30）是照着 HYDE 的耕地序列凑出来的，不是推出来的。代码里它的定值方法一栏填的是"按输出"，标定目标和目标出处都写在旁边。 The exception concerns how much of a cell's land can actually be broken. The model's purely geographic ceiling is 12.5 million km² — land that twentieth-century machinery and fertiliser can farm — while HYDE puts the world's cultivated area in AD 1500 at 2.48 million. The gap is how much land Stone Age and Iron Age people could in fact clear. That figure cannot be sourced from the agronomic literature: a commissioned review confirmed the literature does not express itself as "tool → cultivable ceiling." So this set of coefficients (five steps by metallurgy tier: 0.016, 0.05, 0.10, 0.18, 0.30) was fitted to the HYDE cropland series rather than derived. In the code its provenance field reads "calibrated on output," with the target and the target's source recorded beside it.

其余的改动都不是标定。有三处是算错了口径，修正之后数字才对得上：一是活命下限，原来按"种够自己吃"算，可一个下地的人还要养家里不下地的老小，下限偏低了；二是采集和务农的回报比较，采集那一侧没有扣掉不出去采的老小，两边不是一个口径；三是留种扣除，那个 40% 的幅度来自中世纪欧洲的播种量，被套在了公元前四千年的刀耕火种地上，这一道已经撤掉。 The other changes were not calibration but corrections to how quantities were counted. Three of them: the survival floor was computed as the area needed to feed the cultivator alone, when a working adult must also feed the children and elders who do not work; the comparison between foraging and farming returns diluted the farming side by non-working dependents but not the foraging side; and a 40% seed-corn deduction, whose magnitude came from medieval European sowing rates, had been applied to slash-and-burn plots in 4000 BC. That deduction has been removed.

这一版看得见的变化What is visible in this run

采集者占比从起手的 40% 降到公元 1500 年的 1.2%——农业按地理逐步取代采集，而不是一开始就赢。触发它的是技术：石器档下每个务农的人产出低于采集所得，升到定耕档才翻过来。定耕档从近东按每年约 1.2 公里的速度往外扩散，这个速度跟考古学测出的新石器农业推进波速（约每年 1 公里）同量级，模型没有为此调过任何参数。 The share of the population living by foraging falls from 40% at the start to 1.2% by AD 1500 — agriculture displaces foraging region by region rather than winning from the outset. Technology is what triggers it: at the stone tier a farmer produces less than a forager gathers, and only the settled tier reverses that. The settled tier spreads outward from the Near East at roughly 1.2 km per year, the same order as the archaeologically measured wave of advance for Neolithic farming (about 1 km per year); no parameter was tuned to produce that.

政体从公元前 1500 年左右开始出现，到公元 1500 年有 1,142 个。全程发生 163 次战争，累计战争死亡 1.6 亿。文字铺到 93 个格。城市只有 2 座、城内人口八千——这一层明显没跑起来，成因是城市涌现的门槛要人均余粮达到某个水平，而模型全程的余粮一直贴着温饱线。铸币层全程为零，同样的成因。这两层的缺陷如实留在这里。 Polities begin appearing around 1500 BC and number 1,142 by AD 1500. The run contains 163 wars with a cumulative 160 million war deaths. Writing reaches 93 cells. Cities number only two, holding eight thousand people between them — that layer plainly did not take off, because the city threshold requires per-capita surplus above a level the model never sustains, sitting as it does close to the subsistence line throughout. The coinage layer stays at zero for the same reason. Both shortfalls are left on the record.

政体归属：每个国家一种颜色，灰色是无人统治的格。人口：每格人数取对数着色。耕地：每格熟地面积。口粮比值：每人每天分到的口粮相对于温饱线，1 是刚够吃，红色是饥荒。城市：城内人口。文字与铸币：各格所达的档位。官僚：在编官吏按治下格摊算。 Polities: one hue per state, grey for cells under no rule. Population: people per cell, shaded logarithmically. Cropland: cultivated area per cell. Food ratio: daily ration per person against the subsistence line, where 1 is just enough and red is famine. Cities: urban population. Writing and coinage: the tier each cell has reached. Bureaucracy: officeholders spread across the cells they govern.

怎么读它How to read it

这是模型，不是历史。它能回答的是"给定这套规则，会长出什么样的世界"，不能回答"历史为什么是这样"。人口曲线这次跟历史落在同一量级，但其中"能开多少地"那一步是照历史凑的，所以人口对得上这件事本身不构成对模型的检验。真正没有被标定、因而值得看的是形态：采集怎么退、农业怎么按地理推进、政体从哪里先冒出来、战争打在哪些地方。城市和铸币两层跑不起来，是模型在告诉我们它缺了什么。 This is a model, not history. It can answer what kind of world grows out of a given set of rules; it cannot answer why history went the way it did. The population curve now sits in the same order of magnitude as the record, but the step governing how much land can be broken was fitted to that record — so the population agreement is not itself evidence about the model. What was not calibrated, and therefore worth watching, is shape: how foraging recedes, how farming advances geographically, where polities first appear, where the fighting happens. That cities and coinage never take off is the model telling us what it is missing.
