Fractured-Land 演化可视化Fractured-Land Evolution Visualization

这件作品是一个历史模拟的可视化。模拟围绕"破碎地形"假说展开：地理的破碎程度会不会影响一片大陆最终是长成许多并立的政治体，还是走向一个统一帝国。模型在一张网格化的地图上跑 2500 年，政治体按规则扩张、分裂、竞争；可视化把每一步的版图变化画出来，让人能亲眼看到不同条件下政治格局怎样演化。 This work visualizes a historical simulation built around the "fractured land" hypothesis: does the brokenness of terrain shape whether a continent ends up as many coexisting polities or one unified empire? The model runs for 2,500 simulated years on a gridded map, with polities expanding, splitting, and competing by rule; the visualization draws the changing map at every step, so you can watch political geography evolve under different conditions.

读的时候请记住：这是模型，不是历史本身。模拟的价值在于把假设摆到明面上——改一个条件，看结局怎么变——而不是宣称"历史必然如此"。 A note before you open it: this is a model, not history itself. The value of the simulation is that it puts its assumptions in the open — change one condition and watch the outcome shift — not that it claims history had to happen this way.
