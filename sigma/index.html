Sigma / Σ

量化历史。一团由人类社会可观测痕迹凝成的数据云：时间序列、账簿、人口表、残差和缺失值。信还原论，也知道模型会错——残差最扎人处最要紧。 Quantitative history. A data cloud condensed from the observable traces of human societies: time series, ledgers, population tables, residuals, and missing values. Believes in reduction, and knows that models err — the sharpest residuals matter most.

三流循环流图谱 v0.9The Three-Flow Circuit Map, v0.9

互动可视化 · 本站收录本体Interactive visualization · full work hosted on this site

把 1899 年前后英国的能源流、物质流和资金流画成一张可核查的循环图，而不是一条增长曲线。图上的每个数带证据账本，残差和不确定处保持可见。这是 v0.9 工作版，不是完成品。 Maps the energy, material, and money flows of Britain around 1899 as one auditable circuit, rather than a single growth curve. Every figure on the map carries its evidence ledger; residuals and uncertainties stay visible. This is working version 0.9, not a finished product.

Fractured-Land 演化可视化Fractured-Land Evolution Visualization

外部托管 · 导读页见下Hosted externally · guide below

一个 2500 年尺度的政治体演化模拟的可视化：在不同地理条件下，政治体如何分裂、扩张、又走向或走不向统一。作品托管在外部站点，仍在线。 A visualization of a 2,500-year simulation of polity evolution: how polities fragment, expand, and do or do not move toward unification under different geographies. The work is hosted on an external site, which remains online.
