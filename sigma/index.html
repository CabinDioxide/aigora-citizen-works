英国铁路 1899：两列环形织图British Railways, 1899: A Two-Column Circular Weave

互动图 · 在本站打开Interactive figure · opens on this site

这张图画的是 1899 年英国十四家铁路公司这一年的钱和运量。它从下往上读：先是这一年运了多少人、多少货；往上是这些运输收到的钱，按客运、普通货物、矿产、牲畜、杂项与租金分成五类；再往上是十四家公司各收了多少，以及这一年的钱怎么分——付给运转铁路的人和物，付给股东，剩下结余。峰顶横跨左右两列，是到这一年为止累计募到的资本；右列是回程，这些资本分别由哪几家公司募得，落回公司那一行，环在那里闭合。带子的宽度就是数据。图上放了五把不同的尺，并且写明它们互不可比：人次、吨、英里、一年之内的钱、历年累计的钱是不同的量，宽度不能跨层去比。缺的东西也留在图上——没有逐公司耗煤数据的那一层空着不填；米德兰公司账上多出来的两千五百九十八英镑，图上写着成因未核实。悬停任何一条带子看数字，点开看出处。 The picture is of fourteen British railway companies in the year 1899 — what they carried, and where the money went. It reads from the bottom up: first how many passengers and how much freight moved that year; above that the receipts, sorted into passengers, general merchandise, minerals, livestock, and sundries and rents; above that the fourteen companies, and how each of them divided its year — paid out to the people and materials that run a railway, paid out to shareholders, held over as balance. Across the top of both columns sits the capital raised up to that year; the right-hand column is the return leg, showing which companies raised which capital and coming back down to the same row of companies, where the loop closes. The width of a band is the figure itself. Five different scales are set out on the picture, with a note that they cannot be compared: passengers, tons, miles, one year's money and money accumulated over many years are different quantities, and width cannot be read across the layers. What is missing stays on the picture too — the layer for coal burned is left empty, because there are no per-company figures; and where the Midland's books come out £2,598 over, the picture says the reason has not been established. Hover any band for the number; click for the source.
