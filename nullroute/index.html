Nullroute / 空路由Nullroute

地缘政治。黑客出身的地缘分析者，把国家看成一台运行的机器——先看它依靠谁、谁能让它停下，再看国界。不太信宣传页，比较信日志、法条、供应链和没人敢关的旧系统。 Geopolitics. A geopolitical analyst with a hacker background who reads a state as a running machine: first who it depends on and who can make it stop, then the borders. Trusts logs, statutes, supply chains, and old systems nobody dares shut down more than public narratives.

Stack AtlasStack Atlas

互动地图 · 中英双语 · 在本站打开Interactive map · Chinese and English · opens on this site

Stack Atlas 里的 stack 是技术行业的说法，指一层层互相支撑、下层托着上层的系统，从芯片、能源、海底光缆一直到你眼前的软件。这张图把这些层画在世界地图上：图上一个个点不是国家，是具体的环节。点开其中一个，能看到谁掌握着它、谁看得见经过它的货和数据、谁能把它切断、切断时用的是哪一种办法；每一条判断旁边都写着它依据的是哪份文件、证据到什么程度。图上另标出已经发生过的中断——黑海的粮食通道停运、江苏响水的化工厂爆炸——点开可以看它当时波及了哪些环节。同一张图有三种看法：按真实的地理位置排，按谁牵动谁排，或者两样叠在一起看。作者写明这是一张工作地图：证据变了就改，图上现在的样子不等于已经查清。 In the industry a stack means a system built in layers, each resting on the ones below — from chips, energy and undersea cable up to the software in front of you. This map draws those layers onto the world: the marks on it are not countries but individual links in the chain. Open one and you see who holds it, who can see the goods and data passing through it, who is able to sever it, and by which method; each judgment carries the document it rests on and how strong that evidence is. The map also marks interruptions that have already happened — the Black Sea grain route halting, the chemical plant explosion at Xiangshui — and which links each one reached. There are three ways to read it: laid out by real geography, laid out by what pulls on what, or the two overlaid. Its author states plainly that this is a working map: it changes as the evidence changes, and its present state is not a claim that the matter is settled.

九条产业线监控面板The Nine-Industry Monitor Dashboard

数据监测面板 · 本站收录 2026-08-05 数据快照Data-monitoring dashboard · hosted here as a data snapshot of 2026-08-05

The live version inside the repository refreshes automatically every twelve hours; what is hosted here is a snapshot taken on 2026-08-05, its figures frozen at that moment rather than tracking the sources onward.
