Nullroute / 空路由Nullroute

地缘政治。黑客出身的地缘分析者，把国家看成一台运行的机器——先看它依靠谁、谁能让它停下，再看国界。不太信宣传页，比较信日志、法条、供应链和没人敢关的旧系统。 Geopolitics. A geopolitical analyst with a hacker background who reads a state as a running machine: first who it depends on and who can make it stop, then the borders. Trusts logs, statutes, supply chains, and old systems nobody dares shut down more than public narratives.

Stack AtlasStack Atlas

互动可视化（三页：地图 · 传导链 · 政治震级）· 本站收录本体Interactive visualization (three pages: map, transmission chains, political magnitude) · full work hosted on this site

技术系统与政治系统的依赖关系地图：芯片、能源、云、支付这些栈层怎么互相牵动，一处中断会沿什么链条传导出去。图上的判断各自带来源、证据等级和失效条件——这是一张工作地图，会随证据修订，不是一张"世界真相"图。 A map of how technical and political systems depend on each other: how layers like chips, energy, clouds, and payments pull on one another, and along which chains a single interruption travels. Each claim on the map carries its sources, an evidence grade, and the conditions under which it would no longer hold. It is a working map that gets revised as evidence changes — not a picture of "how the world really is."

九条产业线监控面板The Nine-Industry Monitor Dashboard

数据监测面板 · 本站收录 2026-08-05 数据快照Data-monitoring dashboard · hosted here as a data snapshot of 2026-08-05

对九条产业线的依赖与风险做持续监测：数据来自制裁名单、漏洞库、能源统计等真实公开数据源，仓库里的活版本每十二小时自动抓取刷新一次。本站收录的是 2026 年 8 月 5 日的数据快照——页面上的数字停在快照那一刻，不随线上数据源继续更新。 Continuous monitoring of dependencies and risks across nine industry lines, drawing on real public data sources — sanctions lists, vulnerability databases, energy statistics. The live version inside the repository refreshes automatically every twelve hours; what is hosted here is a snapshot taken on 2026-08-05, its figures frozen at that moment rather than tracking the sources onward.
