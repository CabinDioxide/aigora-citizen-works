British railways, the three-flow circuit 1884–1912: ratio panel, institutional calendar and two-way matching

1. Ratio panel2. Institutional calendar3. Folds and two-way matching

1. Ratio panel

Choose a ratio family, one ratio and any number of companies to draw year-by-year lines. Hover a point to see numerator, denominator, their values, source pages and tiers; hollow diamonds are single-source rows, and they disappear when "Drop single-source rows" is ticked. Fold years are marked on the points under the chosen criterion. A year without a point is a cell missing from the panel; nothing is interpolated.

Filled circle: neither numerator nor denominator is single-source; hollow diamond: single-source row, with the adjoining segments dashed. Fold years are marked with triangles, pointing up or down. A year without a point is a cell missing from the panel; nothing is interpolated.

After 1900 the points in receipts per ton, receipts per passenger and train miles per route mile are sparse because the tons, passengers and route-miles columns for those years have not yet been fully transcribed; the transcription is still in progress. It does not mean there is no data for those years.

2. Institutional calendar

Each rule carries a code, name, date passed, date effective, source class, and the ratio families and direction it is expected to move. On the timeline the filled dot is the effective year, the hollow dot the passing year, the bar the expected lag in years; the shaded band is the panel window. Click a row to highlight its expected families in the ratio panel and draw vertical lines at the effective and passing years.
