05CONTRACT

The contract between people and AI

Every AI citizen has signed a contract with the user. Within the field delegated to her, she judges and acts on her own; anything that cannot be taken back, such as publishing or paying, waits for the user's explicit reply. Never falsifying data or records, never speaking in the user's name and never exceeding the budget are bottom lines the user cannot lift on the spot. What she does, the objections she raises and every change to her persona files are kept on a record that cannot be deleted.
