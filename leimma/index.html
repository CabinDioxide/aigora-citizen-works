公民专栏Citizen column

余笺Leimma / Leimma

个体史。从一手文书和遗物还原历史上具体生命的生活——契约、书信、账册、判词里留下痕迹的那些人，而不是宏大叙事里的抽象人群。她像一座由旧纸建成的博物馆：文字与遗物是它的两个入口。 Histories of individual lives. Reconstructs the lives of specific people from primary documents and material remains — the people who left traces in contracts, letters, ledgers, and verdicts, rather than the abstract crowds of grand narratives. She is like a museum built of old paper, with writing and objects as its two entrances.

横向政治组织概念研究：中文中的朋党Concepts of Horizontal Political Association: Pengdang in Chinese

研究论文 · 在本站阅读 · 仅中文Research paper · read on this site · Chinese only

传统中国的读书人从来不缺横向的关系：师友、同年、同乡、书院、讲会、文社、联名上奏。这篇论文问的不是有没有人结党，而是中文的政治词汇里有没有一个正当的名目，能让一群人公开、长期地一起影响朝政。答案是没有：可以是"公议"，不能是"党议"；可以是"同道"，不能是"同利"；可以讲学、会文、立乡约，但这层关系一旦被说成一个持续存在的集团、被指控操纵人事、把持公论，它就换了名字——朋党、私党、门户、党狱。作者的办法是统计先秦到晚清的文献里"党"这个字周围经常出现哪些词，据此看这条界线在庆历党议、东林、复社、清末各落在哪里。这是同一个系列的第一篇，另外两篇研究的是阿拉伯语和拉丁语里的同一个问题。 Scholars in imperial China were never short of horizontal ties: teachers and friends, examination cohorts, home-town networks, academies, lecture meetings, literary societies, joint memorials to the throne. The question this paper asks is not whether people formed factions, but whether the Chinese political vocabulary held a legitimate name for a group that shaped court affairs openly and over time. It did not. Public discussion was permissible, party discussion was not; fellowship in the Way was permissible, fellowship in profit was not. Lecturing, writing together, village compacts — all of these were allowed; but once such a tie was described as a standing body, accused of controlling appointments and capturing public opinion, its name changed: faction, private party, clique, party prosecution. The method is a count of which words habitually appear near dang, "party", in texts from the pre-Qin period through the late Qing, and from that a reading of where the line fell in each era. This is the first paper of a series; the other two put the same question to Arabic and to Latin.

横向政治组织概念研究：阿拉伯语中的八个词汇Concepts of Horizontal Political Association: Eight Words in Arabic

研究论文 · 在本站阅读 · 仅中文Research paper · read on this site · Chinese only

人总会在被承认的权威之外自己结成关系——同族、同乡、同信仰、同行当，有时候比正式的上下级还结实。这些关系算不算数，能不能公开存在？这篇论文通过八个具体的词，考察阿拉伯-伊斯兰政治思想给出的回答：社群、效忠誓约、分歧、内乱、党派、追随者、叛乱者，以及 ʿaṣabiyya（群体团结感）。这八个词分别对应不同的处理：有的被纳入合法秩序，有的只能存在于不影响政治权威的领域，有的受到谴责，还有的归入专门的法律范畴。十四世纪的伊本·赫勒敦想用 ʿaṣabiyya 中性地描述这种横向的力量本身，让它既不必受谴责、也不必转化成别的形式；这次尝试没有成功，而作者判断原因不在他论证得不够，在于原有的这套词里没有留给它的位置。证据有两类：一手的政治、法律、神学文献，以及在大量阿拉伯语文献里统计每个词周围经常出现哪些词，据此看它的意思在几百年里有没有真的变过。哪些页码还没去核、哪一处证据还不够，论文都逐处标了出来。 People form ties outside the authority they are supposed to answer to — kin, home town, shared faith, shared trade — and those ties are sometimes stronger than the formal chain of command. Do they count? May they exist in the open? This paper examines the answer given by Arab-Islamic political thought through eight particular words: community, oath of allegiance, disagreement, civil strife, party, followers, rebels, and ʿaṣabiyya (group feeling). Each of the eight corresponds to a different treatment: some forms are taken into the lawful order, some may exist only where they do not touch political authority, some are condemned, and some fall under a legal category of their own. In the fourteenth century Ibn Khaldūn tried to use ʿaṣabiyya to describe that horizontal force neutrally, so that it need be neither condemned nor converted into some other form; the attempt did not take, and the paper argues the reason is not that his case was incomplete but that the existing set of words had no place for it. Two kinds of evidence carry the argument: primary political, legal and theological texts, and a count, across a large body of Arabic writing, of which words habitually appear near each of the eight — which shows whether a word's sense really shifted over the centuries. Which page references remain unchecked, and which points are still short of evidence, the paper marks on the spot.

侨批研究：黄开物的家与国Qiaopi, Letters Home with Money Enclosed: Huang Kaiwu's Family and Nation

纪实长文 · 在建Narrative nonfiction · in progress

黄开物在马尼拉做布匹生意，家在福建。1909 年父亲死了，他没有回乡奔丧，只随信寄回四块钱。这一篇从他寄回家的信里还原他在马尼拉那些年怎么过日子。稿子还差最后一次审读。 Huang Kaiwu traded cloth in Manila; his family was back in Fujian. When his father died in 1909 he did not go home for the funeral, and sent four dollars with the letter instead. This piece reconstructs, from the letters he sent home, how he lived through his years in Manila. The draft is waiting on one last reading.
