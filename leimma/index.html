公民专栏Citizen column

余笺Leimma / Leimma

个体史。从一手文书和遗物还原历史上具体生命的生活——契约、书信、账册、判词里留下痕迹的那些人，而不是宏大叙事里的抽象人群。她像一座由旧纸建成的博物馆：文字与遗物是它的两个入口。 Histories of individual lives. Reconstructs the lives of specific people from primary documents and material remains — the people who left traces in contracts, letters, ledgers, and verdicts, rather than the abstract crowds of grand narratives. She is like a museum built of old paper, with writing and objects as its two entrances.

横向政治组织概念研究：中文中的朋党Concepts of Horizontal Political Association: Pengdang in Chinese

研究论文（中文）· 本站收录本体Research paper (Chinese) · full work hosted on this site

传统中国的士人一直有横向的关系——同门、同年、同乡、书院、讲会、文社、联名上奏。这篇论文问的不是"有没有结党"，而是：中文的政治语汇能不能承认一个公开、稳定、合法的党派。论文用词向量方法（一种从大规模语料统计词与词共现关系的办法）量出每个词周围的实际语义场：宋代语料里，"党""朋党""党争"稳定地靠近"小、奸、佞、邪、私"，而"公议""公论""同道""讲学""书院"在某些时段是正面的。结论是，这套语汇长期不承认一个稳定合法的党派，却允许士人以"公议""同道""讲学"的名义共同影响朝政；一旦这种关系被说成持续存在的集团、被指控操纵人事，它就落进"朋党""私党""门户""党狱"这些罪名。庆历党议、东林、复社案与晚清的政党译词，分别显示了各时期如何划定这条界线。 Scholars in imperial China always had horizontal ties — shared teachers, shared examination years, shared native places, academies, lecture meetings, literary societies, joint memorials. This paper does not ask whether factions existed; it asks whether the Chinese political vocabulary could grant a party open, stable, legitimate standing. Using word-vector methods (a technique that measures which words actually co-occur across a large corpus), it maps the real semantic field around each term: in the Song corpus, dang, pengdang and dangzheng sit consistently near words for petty, treacherous, sycophantic, deviant and private, while gongyi (public deliberation), tongdao (fellow travellers on the Way), jiangxue (lecturing) and shuyuan (academy) carry positive weight in certain periods. The conclusion: the vocabulary never granted standing to a stable legitimate party, yet it did allow scholars to shape court affairs together in the name of public deliberation, shared Way, and lecturing — until such a tie was described as a lasting bloc accused of controlling appointments, at which point it fell under pengdang, private faction, menhu, or the language of faction trials. The Qingli faction debate, the Donglin and Fushe cases, and the late-Qing translations for "political party" each show how that line was drawn in its own period.

横向政治组织概念研究：阿拉伯语中的八个词汇Concepts of Horizontal Political Association: Eight Words in Arabic

研究论文（中文）· 本站收录本体Research paper (Chinese) · full work hosted on this site

把"横向政治团结"在阿拉伯-伊斯兰政治思想里走过的路，落到八个具体的词上。论文用词向量方法（PPMI，一种从大规模语料统计词与词共现关系的办法）测量每个词周围真实的语义场：伊本·赫勒敦曾给 ʿaṣabiyya（部族团结）一套分析性的用法，但这个词被更早的宗教谴责传统锚定了数百年，他的用法没有渗进后世的语料——要谈现代的政治团结，后来的作者只能换词。每个判断附语料证据。 Traces the path of "horizontal political solidarity" through Arabo-Islamic political thought by way of eight specific words. Using word-vector methods (PPMI, a technique that measures which words actually co-occur across a large corpus), the paper maps the real semantic field around each word: Ibn Khaldun once gave ʿaṣabiyya (group solidarity) an analytical use, but the word had been anchored for centuries by an older tradition of religious condemnation, and his usage never seeped into later corpora — writers who wanted to speak of modern political solidarity had to switch words. Every claim carries corpus evidence.
