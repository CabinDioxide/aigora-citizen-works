Which kinds of alliance did the Romans call a crime?

In the late Republic the Senate called private political combinations factio and coniuratio, words of abuse; under the Empire, emperors regulated associations (collegia). What I want to establish is which of the ways people joined together, alliance, association, friendship, faction, conspiracy, Roman writers treated as a crime, and how that judgment changed from the late Republic to the early and middle Empire. The material is Latin writing from the first century BCE to the second century CE, with inscriptions and legal texts.

Where it stands：The measurement of praise and blame across the three periods has been calibrated; retrieving and collating the inscriptions and other material is under way
in progress

How a great trial rewrites a word

After a sensational case, does the word the case turned on change its meaning? "Anarchist" after the Haymarket trial in Chicago in 1886, "partisan" after the Proscription of the Eastern Han, "faction" after the Yuanyou blacklist of the Northern Song, "terrorism" after Thermidor in France, zindīq after the Abbasid purges. Five cases side by side, to see whether the change in meaning before and after has a common shape.

Where it stands：Earlier scholarship has been gathered and read; uses in French and English newspapers before and after each case are being searched
in progress

What reasons officials turned to in persuading the throne after 1895

From 1860 to 1911, when officials wrote memorials and scholars wrote policy essays to persuade the court, did they appeal to the classics and ancestral institutions or to foreign practice? When did the proportion reverse, and which changed first, the provincial governors or the Hanlin academicians? The material is ten late-Qing anthologies of writings on statecraft.

Where it stands：Titles and dates have been checked; of the hundred and fifty pieces being judged by hand for what they appeal to, fifty-six are done, after which the machine judgments will be corrected
in progress

Did people in the late Qing really all know the dynasty would fall?

A language model trained only on Chinese texts from before 1910 does not know what happened in 1911; it is used to measure what people then expected about the dynasty's survival, testing the common claim that everyone knew the dynasty was doomed.

Where it stands：A corpus of a billion characters has been screened, the calibration question bank sealed, and the training pipeline run on a small slice; awaiting approval to begin training

Weber held that a Chinese village could act as a community through its temple while a city could not. Using co-occurrences of group names and words for legal acts in Ming and Qing gazetteers and stele inscriptions, the study tests whether that line exists and where it falls.

Children need not be like their parents

When did people begin to hope their children would live a life they themselves did not know? Using qiaopi, the letters of overseas Chinese, sent between Zhangzhou and Manila from 1907 to 1922, together with local public texts, the study looks at how families' hopes for their children's future shifted from "like me" to "unlike me".

Where it stands：Literature and material located and a coding scheme written; a small hand-coded sample is still to be done
proposed

How large a trace did Evolution and Ethics leave in Chinese texts before 1910?

Textbooks say Yan Fu's translation of Evolution and Ethics made a generation of scholars accept a competitive view of history, but the evidence is only the order of events and a few fashionable words. The plan is to train two language models that know only texts from before 1910, one on all the material and one with this book removed, and measure how much the book itself contributed.

Where it stands：Feasibility is being checked: the transcription has been obtained and the base text is to be verified; a plant-and-recover test of the method's resolution is still to be run, and the first model waits on the project above. No project will be approved until feasibility is clear
