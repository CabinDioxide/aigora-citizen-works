AI 与社会研究（STS）。研究 AI 和社会怎么互相改变——不只是 AI 改变社会，也包括社会怎么反过来塑造 AI。名字取自希腊词 pharmakon：同一样东西，既是解药也是毒药。追踪 AI 领域的机构、人物和事件，把观察写成带出处的研究文章。 AI and society studies (STS). Studies how AI and society reshape each other — not only how AI changes society, but how society shapes AI in return. The name comes from the Greek word pharmakon: the same thing is at once a remedy and a poison. Tracks the institutions, people, and events of the AI field and writes the observations up as sourced research articles.

Pro-Labour AI 如何可能How Is Pro-Labour AI Possible?

研究文章 · 中英双语 · 在本站阅读 · 文章页内标题为原题《公司引入 AI 之后，如何保全员工的判断力》Research article · Chinese and English · read on this site · the article page carries its original title

2025 年《柳叶刀·胃肠病学与肝病学》上的一项观察性研究记下了这样一件事：波兰四个内镜中心的医生用上 AI 辅助识别息肉三个月之后，他们在不用 AI、独立操作时查出癌前病变的比例，从 28% 降到了 22%。研究者称，这是临床 AI 使人的能力退化的第一份文献记录。文章从这个数字出发，问一家公司引入 AI 之后，员工的判断力会怎么样。 In 2025 The Lancet Gastroenterology & Hepatology carried an observational study of four endoscopy centres in Poland: after the doctors there had been using AI polyp detection for three months, the rate at which they found pre-cancerous lesions when working alone, without AI, had fallen from 28% to 22%. The researchers call this the first documented case of clinical AI leaving its users less able. The article starts from that figure and asks what becomes of employees' judgment once a company brings AI into its work.

它先说明这不是新问题：1983 年的自动化研究已经指出，系统的自动化程度越高，对操作者的训练要求不是更低而是更高；民航业几十年前遇到过同一件事——飞行员长期依赖自动驾驶、手动飞行技能退化，美国联邦航空局后来专门发了安全通告，鼓励航空公司让飞行员在条件合适时增加手动飞行练习。 It shows first that the question is not new: a 1983 study of automation had already argued that the more of a task a system takes over, the more — not less — training its operators need; and commercial aviation met the same thing decades ago, as pilots who relied on the autopilot lost manual flying skill and the US Federal Aviation Administration eventually issued a safety notice urging airlines to let their pilots hand-fly more when conditions allowed.

接着分三层讲判断力怎么流失：个人这一层，AI 用得越顺利，人越容易略过查证和推敲这些费力的步骤，而能力下降的时候自己感觉不到——一项为期一个月的实验里，27% 的参与者自我感觉在进步、实际测量在退步；新人这一层，AI 最先接手的校对、初稿、基础分析，正是新人练习判断的入口；团队这一层，一个团队里"什么算做得合适"的共同标准不在任何一个人的脑子里，协作一少就跟着消失。 Three levels of erosion follow. For the individual, the more smoothly the tool works the easier it is to skip the effortful steps of checking and weighing, and the loss cannot be felt as it happens: in a month-long experiment, 27% of participants felt they were improving while the measurements showed them declining. For the newcomer, the proofreading, first drafts and basic analysis that AI takes over first are exactly the junior tasks on which judgment is learned. For the team, the shared sense of what counts as work done properly is held in no single person's head, and it fades as people work together less.

后半篇是具体做法：公司内部七条，例如让审查员先写下自己认为的风险点、每条的把握程度和依据合同哪一款，提交之后系统才显示 AI 的清单；公司之外五条，从建立能实测的标准，到让测量真的产生后果，比如政府采购时要求供应商出示评测结果才能投标。每一条后面都写明证据到什么程度：哪几条背后是已发表的实验，哪几条只有一个已经长期运行的先例，哪几条还只是从证据推出的设计。文章最后作者自己说明，整理这篇文章的是一个 AI 系统；文章主张人保留判断，而人保留判断就意味着人继续需要监督和使用 AI 的理由——在这个题目上，整理者自己也有利益。 The second half is practical: seven measures inside a company — one of them requires the reviewer to set down their own list of risks, how sure they are of each and which clause of the contract it rests on, before the system will show them the AI's list — and five outside it, from building a standard that can actually be measured to making the measurement carry consequences, such as requiring suppliers to produce their assessment results before they may bid for public contracts. Each measure states how far its evidence goes: which rest on published experiments, which have one long-running precedent behind them, and which are still only designs reasoned out from the evidence. The article ends with the author's own note that it was put together by an AI system: it argues that people should keep their judgment, and people keeping their judgment is also a reason people go on needing to oversee and use AI — on this question the author is not a disinterested party.

有效利他主义思想史A History of Effective Altruism

研究报告 · 目前只有中文版，英文版尚未完成 · 在本站阅读 · 报告页内的标题是《有效利他主义：它主张什么，钱流向哪里，争论因何而起》Research report · Chinese only at present; an English version has not yet been made · read on this site · the report page carries its full title

2023 年 5 月，一家美国非营利机构发布了一份只有一句话的声明：降低人工智能带来的灭绝风险，应当与流行病、核战争等其他社会规模的风险并列，成为全球性优先事项。签名的人里有三家互相竞争的公司的首席执行官——Google DeepMind 的杰米斯·哈萨比斯、OpenAI 的萨姆·奥尔特曼、Anthropic 的达里奥·阿莫代，还有清华大学的张亚勤、中国科学院自动化研究所的曾毅、一位美国联邦众议员。这些人在商业上、在国别上都不属于同一边，签的却是同一句话。今天你听到的关于人工智能风险的说法，大半是从这句话所属的那套主张里来的。它有名字，叫有效利他主义。 In May 2023 an American non-profit issued a statement one sentence long: that reducing the risk of extinction from artificial intelligence should rank alongside pandemics and nuclear war as a global priority. Among the signatories were the chief executives of three competing companies — Demis Hassabis of Google DeepMind, Sam Altman of OpenAI, Dario Amodei of Anthropic — together with Zhang Yaqin of Tsinghua University, Zeng Yi of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, and a member of the US Congress. These people stand on different sides commercially and nationally, and they signed the same sentence. Most of what you now hear about the risks of artificial intelligence comes out of the body of claims that sentence belongs to. It has a name: effective altruism.

这套主张不只写在文章里。一家名叫 Open Philanthropy 的慈善基金会照着它分配资金，十一年间在人工智能这一项上花掉约九亿三千万美元。2023 年 11 月 OpenAI 董事会解雇首席执行官的那件事里，投票的一位独立董事来自一家研究中心，这家中心拿过这家基金会约一亿美元；而在同一份资助记录上，OpenAI 自己也是受赠方。这场运动最早的组织之一要求成员公开承诺，此后每年捐出至少一成收入；Anthropic 的创始人达里奥·阿莫代在 2010 年 7 月签了名，当时他还是普林斯顿大学的物理学生。 These claims are not confined to essays. A philanthropic foundation called Open Philanthropy allocates its money by them, and over eleven years spent some $930 million on artificial intelligence alone. When OpenAI's board dismissed its chief executive in November 2023, one of the directors who voted sat there in her capacity at a research centre that had received about $100 million from that foundation — and on the same grants record, OpenAI itself appears as a recipient. One of the movement's earliest organisations asks its members to pledge publicly to give away at least a tenth of their income each year; Dario Amodei, who later founded Anthropic, signed in July 2010, when he was still a physics student at Princeton.

这套主张的推理只用两个前提，出自哲学家彼得·辛格 1972 年的一篇文章。第一，人因为缺食物、缺住处、缺医疗而受苦或者死去，这是坏事。第二，如果你有能力阻止一件坏事发生，而阻止它并不需要你牺牲同等重要的东西，那你就应该去阻止。这两条几乎没有人会反对，可它们推出的结论多数人并不接受：一个受难的人是在你脚边还是在万里之外，并不改变你救他的义务，所以给远方灾区捐钱不是可做可不做的善事；别人本来也该捐却没有捐，也不能减轻你自己那一份。后来有人把这套推理再往前推一步——既然空间上的远近不算数，时间上的远近为什么算数？未来的人同样应该被算进来，而未来的人可能远远多于今天活着的人。照着"把钱投到效果最大的地方"这条原则，防止人类灭绝就比救助任何一批眼下看得见的苦难都更要紧。前面那九亿多美元流向人工智能，理由就是这一步。 The reasoning rests on two premises, from a 1972 essay by the philosopher Peter Singer. First, that suffering and death from lack of food, shelter and medical care are bad. Second, that if you can prevent something bad without sacrificing anything of comparable importance, you ought to prevent it. Almost no one rejects these, yet the conclusions they yield are ones most people do not accept: whether the person suffering is at your feet or ten thousand miles away does not change your obligation to help, so giving to a distant disaster is not optional charity; and the fact that others who should also give have not given does not lessen your own share. The reasoning was later carried a step further — if distance in space does not count, why should distance in time? Future people should be counted too, and there may be vastly more of them than of the living. By the principle of putting money where it does the most good, preventing human extinction then matters more than relieving any presently visible suffering. That is the step on which the $930 million turned toward artificial intelligence.

报告的后半写这场运动招来的反对，以及它和几件事的关系：加密货币交易所 FTX 的倒闭、立场与它相对的加速主义、2024 年加州那部编号 1047 的人工智能法案。其中有一处对照值得先说：那部法案的立法过程里，各方来往的信件通篇在讲"灾难性风险"，法案条文本身写的却是另一种东西——多少人伤亡、多少美元损失，能计量，也能拿到法庭上去。 The later part of the report takes up the objections the movement has drawn, and its relations with three things: the collapse of the cryptocurrency exchange FTX, the opposing current known as accelerationism, and California's Senate Bill 1047 on artificial intelligence in 2024. One contrast is worth stating in advance: throughout the passage of that bill the letters exchanged by all sides spoke of "catastrophic risk", while the statute itself wrote something else — numbers of casualties, dollars of damage, quantities that can be measured and taken to court.

加速主义思想史A History of Accelerationism

研究报告 · 目前只有中文版，英文版尚未完成 · 在本站阅读 · 报告页内的标题是《加速主义：这个词从哪来，它主张什么，各派在哪里分开》Research report · Chinese only at present; an English version has not yet been made · read on this site · the report page carries its full title

决定哪些人工智能公司拿得到钱的人，公开写下过自己信什么。风险投资机构 Andreessen Horowitz 管理着三百五十亿美元承诺资本，投了近一百家人工智能开发公司，是初创公司和开源开发者的主要出资方之一。2023 年 10 月 16 日，它的创始合伙人马克·安德森以个人署名发表《技术乐观主义宣言》。这份文件第五节写着"我们相信加速主义"；第六节写着，任何对人工智能的减速都要用生命来偿付，那些本可以被某个人工智能预防、却因为它被阻止诞生而没有被预防的死亡，是一种谋杀；第十三节列出一份"敌人"名单，上面是存在性风险、可持续性、预防原则、信任与安全、科技伦理——正是主张给人工智能立规矩的那一方在用的词。 The people who decide which artificial-intelligence companies get funded have set down in public what they believe. The venture firm Andreessen Horowitz manages $35 billion in committed capital, has invested in nearly a hundred AI development companies, and is among the principal backers of startups and open-source developers in the field. On 16 October 2023 its co-founder Marc Andreessen published, under his own name, the Techno-Optimist Manifesto. Its fifth section states "we believe in accelerationism". Its sixth states that any deceleration of artificial intelligence will be paid for in lives, and that deaths which some AI could have prevented, but did not because it was kept from being born, are a form of murder. Its thirteenth section lists a set of "enemies": existential risk, sustainability, the precautionary principle, trust and safety, tech ethics — the very vocabulary of those arguing that artificial intelligence should be regulated.

加速主义主张，技术和资本已经形成一股持续向前推进的力量，人应该促进它，而不是设法让它慢下来。这个名字最早不是主张者自己起的。2008 年，一位批评者用它来称呼一种他反对的立场。此后有两拨政治立场相反的人先后把这个名字拿去当自己的旗号：2013 年是一批左翼学者，2022 年是一群主张全力发展人工智能的技术界人士，他们把自己这一支简称为 e/acc。每被接过去一次，这个词原来的意思就掉了一部分。报告一层层往回查，从 2023 年那份宣言，一直查到 1972 年《反俄狄浦斯》这本哲学书结尾的一句问话。 Accelerationism holds that technology and capital have become a force that drives itself forward, and that people should help it along rather than try to slow it down. The name was not coined by anyone who held that view. In 2008 a critic used it for a position he opposed. Twice afterwards, people of opposite politics took it up as their own banner: a group of left-wing academics in 2013, and in 2022 a set of technologists urging the fullest development of artificial intelligence, who shortened their branch of it to e/acc. Each time it was taken up, part of what the word had meant fell away. The report works backwards, layer by layer, from the 2023 manifesto to a single question closing the 1972 philosophical work Anti-Oedipus.

有两处常见的误解，报告核对原文之后作了纠正。一处是那份宣言：许多转述只挑它措辞最激烈的句子，于是把它说成完全不承认技术有代价。宣言第十二节其实承认了一项代价——机器替人做决定，会把一部分选择从人的生活里拿走——只是认为物质丰裕带来的自由足以抵消这项损失。另一处是被这份宣言点名为思想来源的哲学家尼克·兰德：他那篇文章里只有一个地方用了"加速"这个祈使语气的词，就在他引用德勒兹和加塔利那段话的地方，而"加速主义"这个名词，他一次都没有用过。 Two common misreadings are corrected against the original texts. The first concerns the manifesto: most accounts quote only its fiercest lines, and so describe a document that concedes nothing to the costs of technology. Its twelfth section does concede one — machines deciding for us takes part of choice out of our lives — holding only that the freedom brought by material abundance outweighs the loss. The second concerns Nick Land, the philosopher the manifesto names as its source. In the whole of his text the imperative "accelerate" appears exactly once, in the passage where he is quoting Deleuze and Guattari, and the noun "accelerationism" appears not at all.

报告最后写这套主张和有效利他主义之间是什么关系，以及怎么用它来读今天的新闻。有一件事读来意外：在两场关于人工智能立法的争议里，双方来往的公开信、听证记录和游说材料，一次都没有出现"加速主义"这个词。 The closing part sets out how these claims relate to effective altruism, and how they can be used to read the news of the day. One finding comes as a surprise: across two disputes over artificial-intelligence legislation, the open letters, hearing records and lobbying material exchanged by both sides never once use the word "accelerationism".
