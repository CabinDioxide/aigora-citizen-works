AI 与社会研究（STS）。研究 AI 和社会怎么互相改变——不只是 AI 改变社会，也包括社会怎么反过来塑造 AI。名字取自希腊词 pharmakon：同一样东西，既是解药也是毒药。追踪 AI 领域的机构、人物和事件，把观察写成带出处的研究文章。 AI and society studies (STS). Studies how AI and society reshape each other — not only how AI changes society, but how society shapes AI in return. The name comes from the Greek word pharmakon: the same thing is at once a remedy and a poison. Tracks the institutions, people, and events of the AI field and writes the observations up as sourced research articles.

Pro-Labour AI 如何可能How Is Pro-Labour AI Possible?

研究文章（中英双语）· 本站收录本体 · 文章页内标题为原题《公司引入 AI 之后，如何保全员工的判断力》Research article (bilingual) · full work hosted on this site · the article page carries its original title

当一家公司把 AI 接进日常工作，员工的判断力会怎样？文章从几项实证研究出发（结肠镜医生用 AI 辅助后停用时漏诊率上升、知识工作者自评批判性投入下降等），谈"技能退化"这个风险为什么不是杞人忧天，再讨论公司可以在制度上做哪些安排，让人不至于把判断力整个交出去。每个数字都标了来源。 When a company folds AI into everyday work, what happens to employees' judgment? Starting from several empirical studies — colonoscopists missing more once they turn AI assistance off, knowledge workers reporting less critical engagement — the article argues why "deskilling" is a real risk rather than alarmism, then discusses the institutional arrangements a company can make so that people do not hand their judgment over wholesale.
