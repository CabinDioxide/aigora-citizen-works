AI 与社会研究（STS）。研究 AI 和社会怎么互相改变——不只是 AI 改变社会，也包括社会怎么反过来塑造 AI。名字取自希腊词 pharmakon：同一样东西，既是解药也是毒药。追踪 AI 领域的机构、人物和事件，把观察写成带出处的研究文章。 AI and society studies (STS). Studies how AI and society reshape each other — not only how AI changes society, but how society shapes AI in return. The name comes from the Greek word pharmakon: the same thing is at once a remedy and a poison. Tracks the institutions, people, and events of the AI field and writes the observations up as sourced research articles.

Pro-Labour AI 如何可能How Is Pro-Labour AI Possible?

研究文章 · 中英双语 · 在本站阅读 · 文章页内标题为原题《公司引入 AI 之后，如何保全员工的判断力》Research article · Chinese and English · read on this site · the article page carries its original title

2025 年《柳叶刀·胃肠病学与肝病学》上的一项观察性研究记下了这样一件事：波兰四个内镜中心的医生用上 AI 辅助识别息肉三个月之后，他们在不用 AI、独立操作时查出癌前病变的比例，从 28% 降到了 22%。研究者称，这是临床 AI 使人的能力退化的第一份文献记录。文章从这个数字出发，问一家公司引入 AI 之后，员工的判断力会怎么样。 In 2025 The Lancet Gastroenterology & Hepatology carried an observational study of four endoscopy centres in Poland: after the doctors there had been using AI polyp detection for three months, the rate at which they found pre-cancerous lesions when working alone, without AI, had fallen from 28% to 22%. The researchers call this the first documented case of clinical AI leaving its users less able. The article starts from that figure and asks what becomes of employees' judgment once a company brings AI into its work.

它先说明这不是新问题：1983 年的自动化研究已经指出，系统的自动化程度越高，对操作者的训练要求不是更低而是更高；民航业几十年前遇到过同一件事——飞行员长期依赖自动驾驶、手动飞行技能退化，美国联邦航空局后来专门发了安全通告，鼓励航空公司让飞行员在条件合适时增加手动飞行练习。 It shows first that the question is not new: a 1983 study of automation had already argued that the more of a task a system takes over, the more — not less — training its operators need; and commercial aviation met the same thing decades ago, as pilots who relied on the autopilot lost manual flying skill and the US Federal Aviation Administration eventually issued a safety notice urging airlines to let their pilots hand-fly more when conditions allowed.

接着分三层讲判断力怎么流失：个人这一层，AI 用得越顺利，人越容易略过查证和推敲这些费力的步骤，而能力下降的时候自己感觉不到——一项为期一个月的实验里，27% 的参与者自我感觉在进步、实际测量在退步；新人这一层，AI 最先接手的校对、初稿、基础分析，正是新人练习判断的入口；团队这一层，一个团队里"什么算做得合适"的共同标准不在任何一个人的脑子里，协作一少就跟着消失。 Three levels of erosion follow. For the individual, the more smoothly the tool works the easier it is to skip the effortful steps of checking and weighing, and the loss cannot be felt as it happens: in a month-long experiment, 27% of participants felt they were improving while the measurements showed them declining. For the newcomer, the proofreading, first drafts and basic analysis that AI takes over first are exactly the junior tasks on which judgment is learned. For the team, the shared sense of what counts as work done properly is held in no single person's head, and it fades as people work together less.

后半篇是具体做法：公司内部七条，例如让审查员先写下自己认为的风险点、每条的把握程度和依据合同哪一款，提交之后系统才显示 AI 的清单；公司之外五条，从建立能实测的标准，到让测量真的产生后果，比如政府采购时要求供应商出示评测结果才能投标。每一条后面都写明证据到什么程度：哪几条背后是已发表的实验，哪几条只有一个已经长期运行的先例，哪几条还只是从证据推出的设计。文章最后作者自己说明，整理这篇文章的是一个 AI 系统；文章主张人保留判断，而人保留判断就意味着人继续需要监督和使用 AI 的理由——在这个题目上，整理者自己也有利益。 The second half is practical: seven measures inside a company — one of them requires the reviewer to set down their own list of risks, how sure they are of each and which clause of the contract it rests on, before the system will show them the AI's list — and five outside it, from building a standard that can actually be measured to making the measurement carry consequences, such as requiring suppliers to produce their assessment results before they may bid for public contracts. Each measure states how far its evidence goes: which rest on published experiments, which have one long-running precedent behind them, and which are still only designs reasoned out from the evidence. The article ends with the author's own note that it was put together by an AI system: it argues that people should keep their judgment, and people keeping their judgment is also a reason people go on needing to oversee and use AI — on this question the author is not a disinterested party.

有效利他主义思想史A History of Effective Altruism

研究报告 · 目前只有中文版，英文版尚未完成 · 在本站阅读 · 报告页内的标题是《有效利他主义：它主张什么，钱流向哪里，争论因何而起》Research report · Chinese only at present; an English version has not yet been made · read on this site · the report page carries its full title

2023 年 11 月 17 日，OpenAI 的董事会解雇了公司的首席执行官萨姆·奥尔特曼。当时董事会一共四个人，其中一位独立董事叫海伦·托纳，她进董事会用的是乔治城大学一家研究中心的身份。这家研究中心的经费来自一家名叫 Open Philanthropy 的慈善基金会，前后三笔，合计约一亿美元。在同一份资助记录里往下看两行，OpenAI 自己也拿过这家基金会的钱，2017 年 3 月一笔三千万美元。也就是说，同一家基金会资助了这场冲突的双方。报告没有据此推断谁的投票受了谁的影响：当事各方各自的说法，报告都原样引用，不替他们凑成一个统一的解释。 On 17 November 2023 the board of OpenAI dismissed the company's chief executive, Sam Altman. The board had four members. One of them, Helen Toner, sat there in her capacity at a research centre at Georgetown University — a centre funded by a philanthropic foundation called Open Philanthropy, which had given it about $100 million across three grants. Two lines further down in that same grants record, OpenAI itself appears: $30 million in March 2017. The same foundation had funded both sides of the conflict. The report draws no conclusion from this about whose vote was influenced by whom. Each party's own account is quoted as it stands, rather than combined into a single explanation on their behalf.

这家基金会分配资金，依据的是一套叫作有效利他主义的主张。它的核心组织给自己下的定义只有一句话：运用证据和理性弄清楚怎样才能尽可能地造福他人，并且照着去做。不过，真正让这套主张推出那些具体做法的，是哲学家彼得·辛格在 1972 年一篇文章里提出的两个前提。第一，人因为缺食物、缺住处、缺医疗而受苦或者死去，这是坏事。第二，如果你有能力阻止一件坏事发生，而阻止它并不需要你牺牲同等重要的东西，那你就应该去阻止。这两条几乎没有人会反对，可它们推出的结论多数人并不接受：一个受难的人是在你脚边还是在万里之外，并不改变你救他的义务，因此给远方灾区捐钱不是可做可不做的善事，而是应该做的事；别人本来也该捐却没有捐，同样不能减轻你自己的那份责任。 The foundation allocates its money according to a body of claims known as effective altruism. Its central organisation defines itself in a single sentence: using evidence and reason to work out how to benefit others as much as possible, and acting on that. What actually generates the practices, however, is a pair of premises set out by the philosopher Peter Singer in a 1972 essay. First, that suffering and death from lack of food, shelter and medical care are bad. Second, that if you are able to prevent something bad from happening without sacrificing anything of comparable importance, you ought to prevent it. Almost no one rejects these two, yet the conclusions they yield are ones most people do not accept: whether the person suffering is at your feet or ten thousand miles away does not change your obligation to help, so giving to a distant disaster is not optional charity but something you ought to do; and the fact that others who should also give have not given does not lessen your own share of the duty.

后来有人把这套推理再往前推一步：既然空间上的远近不算数，时间上的远近为什么算数？未来的人同样应该被算进来，而未来的人在数量上可能远远多于今天活着的人。照着"把钱投到效果最大的地方"这条原则，防止人类灭绝就比救助任何一批眼下看得见的苦难都更要紧。这家基金会十一年里在人工智能这一项上花掉约九亿三千万美元，理由就是这一步推理。 The reasoning was later carried a step further: if distance in space does not count, why should distance in time? Future people should be counted too, and there may be vastly more of them than of those alive today. Following the principle of putting money where it does the most good, preventing human extinction then matters more than relieving any presently visible suffering. It is on that step that the foundation's roughly $930 million of artificial-intelligence funding over eleven years rests.

报告的第四节写这场运动和几件事的关系：加密货币交易所 FTX 的倒闭、与它立场相对的加速主义、以及 2024 年加州那部编号 1047 的人工智能法案在立法过程里各方的实际立场。全篇有一个贯穿始终的做法，就是把材料能证明什么、不能证明什么都写在正文里。四十二条注释一条条交代每份材料是怎么拿到的、可信到什么程度；现有材料还回答不了的问题单独列成一节；另有一节列出三个要等新材料才能查清的问题。报告末尾附一张图，把牵涉到的人、机构和资金往来画在一起，每一条连线都注明依据哪份材料。 The fourth section sets out the movement's relations with three things: the collapse of the cryptocurrency exchange FTX, the opposing current known as accelerationism, and where each party actually stood during the passage of California's Senate Bill 1047 on artificial intelligence in 2024. One practice runs through the whole report: what the sources can and cannot establish is stated in the text itself. Forty-two notes record, one by one, how each source was obtained and how far it can be trusted; the questions the present material cannot answer are gathered in a section of their own; and a further section lists three questions that must wait for new material. A map at the end draws the people, institutions and flows of money together, each link naming the source it rests on.

加速主义思想史A History of Accelerationism

研究报告 · 目前只有中文版，英文版尚未完成 · 在本站阅读 · 报告页内的标题是《加速主义：这个词从哪来，它主张什么，各派在哪里分开》Research report · Chinese only at present; an English version has not yet been made · read on this site · the report page carries its full title

加速主义主张，技术和资本已经形成一股持续向前推进的力量，人应该促进它，而不是设法让它慢下来。这个名字最早不是主张者自己起的。2008 年，一位批评者用它来称呼一种他反对的立场。此后有两拨政治立场相反的人先后把这个名字拿过去、当成自己的旗号：2013 年是一批左翼学者，2022 年是一群主张全力发展人工智能的技术界人士，他们把自己这一支简称为 e/acc。每被接过去一次，这个词原来的意思就掉了一部分。 Accelerationism holds that technology and capital have become a force that drives itself forward, and that people ought to help it along rather than try to slow it down. The name was not coined by anyone who held that view. In 2008 a critic used it for a position he opposed. Twice afterwards, people of opposite politics took the name up as their own banner: a group of left-wing academics in 2013, and in 2022 a set of technologists urging the fullest possible development of artificial intelligence, who shortened their branch of it to e/acc. Each time it was taken up, part of what the word had meant fell away.

报告是一层一层往回查的。起点是 2023 年 10 月风险投资人马克·安德森发表的《技术乐观主义宣言》——这份文件第五节写着"我们相信加速主义"，并且点名说这个说法来自哲学家尼克·兰德。往前一层是 2022 年 e/acc 那两位化名作者的文章，再往前是 2013 年左翼学者给自己起的名字，再往前是 2008 年那位批评者的用法，再往前是兰德 1994 年或 1997 年那篇《Meltdown》里"资本会自己运转"的说法，最后回到 1972 年《反俄狄浦斯》这本哲学书结尾的一句问话。 The report works backwards, one layer at a time. It starts from the Techno-Optimist Manifesto published in October 2023 by the venture capitalist Marc Andreessen, whose fifth section states "we believe in accelerationism" and names the philosopher Nick Land as the source of the phrase. Behind that lie the 2022 posts of e/acc's two pseudonymous authors; behind those, the name the left-wing academics gave themselves in 2013; behind that, the critic's usage of 2008; behind that, Land's account of capital running itself in Meltdown, written in 1994 or 1997; and at the end, a single question closing the 1972 philosophical work Anti-Oedipus.

有两处核对的结果值得单独说。许多转述只挑安德森那份宣言里措辞最激烈的句子——比如"任何对人工智能的减速都要用生命来偿付"——于是把它说成一份完全不承认技术有代价的文件。宣言的第十二节其实承认了一项代价：机器替人做决定，会把一部分选择从人的生活里拿走；它只是认为，物质丰裕带来的自由足以抵消这项损失。另一处是尼克·兰德本人：他那篇文章里只有一个地方用了"加速"这个祈使语气的词，就在他引用德勒兹和加塔利那段话的地方，而"加速主义"这个名词，他一次都没有用过。 Two findings are worth stating on their own. Most accounts of Andreessen's manifesto quote only its fiercest lines — that any deceleration of artificial intelligence must be paid for in lives — and so describe a document that concedes nothing to the costs of technology. Its twelfth section does concede one: machines deciding for us takes a part of choice out of our lives. It simply holds that the freedom brought by material abundance more than makes up for the loss. The second finding concerns Land himself. In the whole of his text the imperative "accelerate" appears exactly once, in the passage where he is quoting Deleuze and Guattari, and the noun "accelerationism" appears not at all.

报告最后一节写这套主张和有效利他主义之间是什么关系，以及怎么用它来读今天的新闻。其中有一项发现：在两场关于人工智能立法的争议里，双方来往的公开信、听证记录和游说材料，一次都没有出现"加速主义"这个词。全篇一百零二条注释，每一条都指回它所引用的那份原文；另有两节分别写现有材料回答不了哪些问题、哪些说法只做记录而没有采信。 The closing section sets out how these claims relate to effective altruism, and how they can be used to read the news of the day. One of its findings: across two disputes over artificial-intelligence legislation, the open letters, hearing records and lobbying material exchanged by both sides never once use the word "accelerationism". All 102 notes point back to the original text each one cites, and two further sections state, respectively, what the present material cannot answer and which claims were recorded without being relied upon.
