## Argument
The cognitive demands of policing lead officers to jump to conclusions. A training programme ("Sit-D") that teaches officers to consider alternative interpretations of situations improves decisions in the field.

## Design
RCT with 2,070 Chicago Police Department officers (abstract). Lab assessments plus administrative outcomes about four months after training (p. 5).

## Important for how the project uses it
- Sit-D is a **human training programme** that uses debriefed simulator exercises (p. 12). **It is not an AI or algorithmic tool.** The commissioning party's notes describe it, via volume ch. 11, as an example of algorithms "providing practice". The paper must describe it accurately: as evidence that structured practice with feedback changes field decisions, cited because ch. 11 places it among "algorithm as practice" uses.
