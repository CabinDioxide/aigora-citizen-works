## Argument
ADP payroll data show no economy-wide displacement. Employment of 22–25-year-olds in AI-exposed occupations has diverged downward from less-exposed peers, mainly through reduced hiring. The decline is concentrated where AI use substitutes for tasks. The authors stress the results are descriptive, not causal (p. 1; p. 4).

## Mechanism statements relevant to the project (p. 3)
- Declines are concentrated where AI usage automates. Complementary usage goes with flat or rising employment, especially for experienced workers.
- "Occupations that involve tacit knowledge see faster employment growth for experienced workers." This is directly relevant to the premise and to condition 5, but it was read only in the introduction summary.

## Caveats (pp. 1, 3–4)
- Some divergent pre-trends.
- Attenuation when controlling for occupational education.
- More pronounced in ADP than in national surveys.
- Not causal.
