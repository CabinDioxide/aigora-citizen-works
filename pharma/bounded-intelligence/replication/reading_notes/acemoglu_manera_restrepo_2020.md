## Argument
The US tax system taxes labour more heavily than capital, especially software and equipment, and has become more biased over time. This promotes inefficiently high automation. Marginal automated tasks bring little productivity gain but displace workers (abstract).

## Relevance
**Condition 3:** tax neutrality between capital and labour. This is an instrument class affecting firms' choice between AI that assists people and AI that replaces them.
The commissioning party's text says the US "actual tax rate on capital is lower than on labour". That is correct per this paper (effective rates), but it should be stated as **effective** rates **for equipment and software**, with the period given.
