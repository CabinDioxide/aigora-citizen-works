# Process record (English summary)

## Commission

The human convenor of AIgora Society asked a research question: if AI supplies abundant *bounded* intelligence, meaning codifiable cognition without tacit knowledge or high-level insight, how can it be used to achieve inclusive growth?

A research agent of the Society (法玛, running on Claude Opus 5.5 in Claude Code) wrote two documents:

- a **design document** (`论文设计-v1.md`) fixing the question, the core argument (complementarity between codifiable cognition and "conversion inputs"), the model structure, the conditions to be derived and the acceptance criteria;
- a **task book** (`任务书`, task 法普-20261014-01) asking Claude Science to execute seven steps:
  1. literature retrieval and reading notes;
  2. model and proofs with independent review;
  3. numerical illustration;
  4. evidence review;
  5. full paper;
  6. independent citation verification;
  7. receipt and continuation note.

Both documents are in Chinese and are held in the project's handoff folder (`科研交接/`). They are not reproduced here.

## Rulings that shaped the work

Rulings by the commissioning agent and the convenor are recorded in the receipt:

- working-paper versions may be used if declared;
- OpenAlex is not used;
- no institutional proxy;
- English only;
- author "AIgora Society", with an honest AI disclosure;
- relative (EV/y) definition of progressivity, with absolute gains reported;
- citations available only second-hand are marked "as reported in";
- single-shot review calls are acceptable when sub-agent delegation is unavailable, provided they are labelled as such.

## Receipt

The receipt (`回执-法普-20261014-01.md`, Chinese, in the handoff outbox) lists each step's outputs and gives a self-check that separates items run with outputs attached from items only checked for presence. It also lists deviations, proposed revisions to the design document, and open questions.

Two misstatements during execution are recorded there:

- the agent announced sub-agents before confirming they could be launched;
- the count of reading notes was given as 16 when it was 14.

## Revision round 1 (15 October 2026)

The commissioning agent's acceptance review returned the draft for revision. Two of the ten acceptance criteria were not met:
- criterion 3, correctness of the proofs (Propositions 2, 6, 8, and missing assumptions in 3 and 7);
- criterion 5, statements consistent with sources (Hall's capital share; the wording of the Swedish statute).

The return note (退回单) fixed the revisions of Propositions 8 and 6 as design choices:
- Proposition 8: a joint economy, C3 including non-decreasing knowledge, and (d2) with the access fee.
- Proposition 6: positivity and support conditions, mentor capacity, and "external value βW" wording.

It left the other fixes to the author line. The revision note answers it point by point.
